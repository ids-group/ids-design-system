Page composition - four regions, and three of them are components

The individual patterns below each answer one screen. This one answers the layer above: which region holds what, in which order, and how to tell whether a composition is right before anyone reviews it. The grid itself — tiers, spans, the canonical splits — is in Foundations → Layout.

Nothing on a page sits outside one of these. The sidenav is reserved OUTSIDE the page grid; the topbar is global, not per-page. Both are drawn dashed here for that reason.

ds-sidenav rail reserved first, outside the grid
ds-topbar global
ds-page-header breadcrumbs · title · meta · actions · tabs
ds-filter-bar only if the page has data to filter
the content, on .page-grid
ds-page-footer optional
