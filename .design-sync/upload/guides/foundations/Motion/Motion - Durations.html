Motion - durations

Six durations, two loop durations, seven eases. All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion : one-shot durations collapse to 0; loops (--duration-loop-*) slow down instead of stopping. Pair durations with the right easing per direction: decelerate for enter, accelerate for exit.

Hover over each card to play the animation. Each puck travels the same distance over its duration with a shared easing.

--duration-instant 80ms
Hover / instant feedback
--duration-fast 120ms
Toggles, micro-state
--duration-base 180ms
Default UI transitions
--duration-slow 260ms
Drawer, toast enter
--duration-slower 400ms
Page transitions, large reveals
--duration-slowest 600ms
A chart mark growing or drawing on

--duration-loop-slow 1400ms
