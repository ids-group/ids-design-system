Motion - durations

Duration, easing and role tokens behind every transition.

Hover to play. Under prefers-reduced-motion one-shot durations drop to 0; loops (--duration-loop-*) slow down.

--duration-instant 80ms
Hover / instant feedback
--duration-fast 120ms
Toggles, micro-state
--duration-base 180ms
Default UI transitions
--duration-slow 260ms
Drawer, toast enter
--duration-slower 400ms
Page transitions, large reveals
--duration-slowest 600ms
A chart mark growing or drawing on
--duration-loop-fast 700ms
Loop: spinner turn
--duration-loop-slow 1400ms
Loop: indeterminate bar
