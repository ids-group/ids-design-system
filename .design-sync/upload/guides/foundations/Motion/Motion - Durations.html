Motion - durations

Five durations, five eases. All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion — durations collapse to 0 so components don't need to opt in. Pair durations with the right easing per direction: decelerate for enter, accelerate for exit.

Hover over each card to play the animation. Each puck travels the same distance over its duration with a shared easing.
