# Motion - entrance presets · customizing a surface

Every DS surface names its entrance through a token, so a consumer can retune or replace it without touching the component. Pick a preset below, then open the dropdown (a portaled panel) and replay the alert (an inline surface) — both read the same vocabulary.

The knobs are the same on every surface — --ds-<surface>-enter-keyframes, -enter-duration, -enter-easing, plus -enter-offset or -enter-scale for the travel/scale a preset reads. Surfaces that animate on close also take -leave-duration and -leave-easing. Set them globally, per theme, or on a single component instance — portaled panels forward them onto their overlay pane on open. See the token reference for the full per-surface list.
