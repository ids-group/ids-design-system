Opacity & alpha tokens - literal alphas in tokens (reference)

Non-tokenised alphas used inside other tokens. Don't reach for these directly — consume the parent token (--shadow-modal, --focus-field, --surface-scrim ).

Alpha Where it appears
--shadow-tint-h/s/l Channel-split substrate for --shadow-1..--shadow-4. Light: 225 / 39% / 7%. Re-skin propagates through HSL channels.
--focus-field-alpha (0.22) Field focus halo alpha; composes hsla(--primary-h, --primary-s, --primary-l, …) inside --focus-field. Dark themes may override it.
--surface-scrim Modal / drawer backdrop. Consumes --opacity-scrim (0.55) over neutral substrate.
