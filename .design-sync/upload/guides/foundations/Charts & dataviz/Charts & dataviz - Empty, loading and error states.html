Charts & dataviz - empty, loading and error states

Every chart draws these: empty data shows emptyText and [error] errorText . Unset copy comes from DS_INTL.chart .

  • [loading] , no data - skeleton in the chart's shape.
  • [loading] , data on screen - plot under the ds-loading-overlay scrim and spinner; caption uncovered.
  • Skeleton on every load - pass empty data while loading.
  • skeletonCount (default 5 ) - skeleton marks while the data's count is unknown: columns on ds-chart, ds-column-chart ; slices on ds-pie-chart ; stages on ds-funnel-chart ; steps on ds-waterfall-chart .
