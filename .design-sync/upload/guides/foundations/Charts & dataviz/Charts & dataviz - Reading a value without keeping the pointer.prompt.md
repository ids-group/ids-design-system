# Charts & dataviz - reading a value without keeping the pointer still

An inert tooltip cannot be read at high zoom, cannot have its number selected, and cannot be cleared without moving the pointer somewhere else. WCAG 1.4.13 asks for all three, so every chart panel here takes the pointer and Esc closes it. Once cleared it stays cleared until you visit another mark, so pressing Esc to get the panel off the screen actually gets it off the screen.

A chart answers a resting pointer, not a passing one: the tooltip, the highlight and the dimming wait DS_CONFIG.chart.hoverDelayMs, 500 by default, before they show. Once the chart has answered, the next mark answers at once until the pointer leaves the chart. Focus, a tap and a click that pins answer at once. Set it to 0 to answer at once, which is also what a test that hovers and asserts in the same tick needs.

An unheld panel goes when the pointer does - one left behind sits over the plot you have moved on to. tooltipLingerMs is how long it stays after the pointer leaves, and it is 0. Raise it only where a panel holds something to REACH — a link, a figure, a selectable string — which is what WCAG's hoverable clause is about; the built-in panel is one line of text and has nothing in it to reach. The panel is portaled out of the chart, so crossing the gap to it at an ordinary pointer speed takes about 140. Set it per chart, or on DS_CONFIG.chart.tooltipLingerMs for an app whose tooltips all carry one.

A click on a mark holds it by default, on every chart: the mark keeps its tooltip, its highlight and, with dimOthers, the dimming of everything else, so a comparison survives the pointer leaving the chart entirely. A pin disc marks the held mark, and every chart but a composed ds-chart shows an Unpin control at its top end. A second click, Esc or a press elsewhere releases it, and the click output fires either way, because pinning is a state rather than a handler. A key never pins: Enter and Space on a focused mark fire its click output and hold nothing. Set pinOnClick="false" on one chart (cursorPin="false" on ds-chart), or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for an app, to keep marks unheld. On ds-line-chart a press holds the whole category under the cursor.
