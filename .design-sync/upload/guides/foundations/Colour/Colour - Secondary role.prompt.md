# Colour - secondary role

The --secondary family is the DS's secondary colour role — a complementary tone for non-interactive emphasis (active rails, decorative indicators, accent pills). It defaults to the orange family, distinct from --primary; override --secondary-h/s/l to reskin it. Secondary is not a second CTA — primary action stays on --primary. Opt-in adoptions: <ds-tabs class="secondary">, <ds-status-dot [style.--ds-status-dot-color]="'var(--secondary)'">, sidenav active rail, stat delta neutral.
