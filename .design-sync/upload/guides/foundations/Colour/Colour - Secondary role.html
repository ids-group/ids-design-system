Colour - secondary role

Three layers: raw ramps (50–950) → semantic role tokens (surface, border, text, status) → component tokens. Components only consume role tokens, so a brand re-skin propagates without code changes.

The --secondary family is the DS's secondary colour role — a complementary tone for non-interactive emphasis (active rails, decorative indicators, accent pills). Defaults at :root alias --secondary to --primary so unbranded apps render unchanged; per-brand overrides introduce a true secondary hue. Secondary is not a second CTA — primary action stays on --primary . Opt-in adoptions: <ds-tabs class="secondary"> , <ds-status-dot [style.--ds-status-dot-color]="'var(--secondary)'"> , sidenav active rail, stat delta neutral.
