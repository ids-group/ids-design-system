Radius - nesting: inner < control < panel, and never equal

A rounded child needs a SMALLER radius than its parent, or its corner bulges.

Field in a card
panel 12px · child 8px — correct
Field in a card
panel 12px · child 12px — the corner bulges
  • Where roles do not cover a value: inner radius = outer radius minus the padding between
  • --radius-flat means "deliberately square", not "unset"; button-group middles and in-range calendar days use it
