Radius - scale

Five-stop scale (0 / sm / md / lg / 2xl) plus pill — but reach for a ROLE, not a stop: --ds-radius-inner , --ds-radius-control , --ds-radius-panel and --ds-radius-pill are what components actually resolve, and they nest in that order. Retheming corners means moving the four roles, not editing components. --radius-pill is themable via --radius-pill-value .

One tile per stop. Top-left corner is the truthful one — tiles share the same dimensions so adjacent steps stay comparable.
