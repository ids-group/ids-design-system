Layout - breakpoints

Breakpoints, page grid and content caps.

@media conditions cannot read custom properties; use the Sass aliases. After @use 'styles/breakpoints' as bp write @include bp.up(md) or bp.down(sm) (up inclusive, down exclusive), or read bp.$bp-md directly. --bp-* properties below serve regular rules and JS.

--bp-xs
480 px
Phone portrait — Team app on the go
--bp-sm
640 px
Phone landscape, small tablets
--bp-md
768 px
Tablet portrait — Field-ops dashboard
--bp-lg
1024 px
Laptop / tablet landscape — minimum planner dashboard
--bp-xl
1441 px
Wide desktop — page-grid wide tier (12 cols, 1280 px content cap)
--bp-2xl
1536 px
Wide desktop — Bulk inventory matrix benefits from this
