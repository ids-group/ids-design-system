Heat map - default

A matrix of readings coloured by magnitude - depot by weekday, hour by date, queue by region. The least code per unit of insight of anything in the family, and the strictest about what has to sit beside it.

Rows down the side, columns across the top, one reading per cell. Magnitude is read from the STEP between two colours, which is why the heat scale spans the full lightness range rather than a band of near-neighbours. It is a separate scale from the funnel's ramp, which stays in the middle of the range for the opposite reason.
