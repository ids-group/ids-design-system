# Heat map - a hole is not a low reading

A null draws as the surface, not as the heat scale's coldest step, and it is left out of the scale entirely. The coldest step is a real measurement; painting "we never measured this" with it states a figure nobody recorded. Depot F below opened on Thursday and nobody reports on Sunday - neither reads as a quiet day.

Full API: `HeatmapChart.d.ts`. How to use the component anywhere: `HeatmapChart.prompt.md`.
