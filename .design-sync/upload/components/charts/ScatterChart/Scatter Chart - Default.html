Scatter chart - default

Two continuous quantities against each other, such as weight against carriage cost.

  • Always set xLabel and yLabel .
  • Marks are semi-transparent, so pile-ups read darker: --ds-scatter-chart-dot-opacity (0.72), radius --ds-scatter-chart-dot-radius .
  • Marks enter together, with no stagger.
  • tickCount hints both axes; ticks snap to a round step.
  • tooltip="none" drops the panel; dimming: Charts foundations .
