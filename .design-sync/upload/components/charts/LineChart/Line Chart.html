Missing readings

A null reading is a hole, not a zero, and gaps decides what it draws: break , the default, stops the line and picks it up after; zero draws through the baseline. Any non-finite value takes the same route, so an upstream NaN lands here too.

Second axis · rounded (default)

A series in other units sets axis: 'secondary' and is measured against the right-hand scale; yAxisMode reconciles the two. Rounded keeps one set of gridlines and rounds the secondary step up, so that line stops short of the top.

Area fill

area: true fills the series down to its axis baseline, which asserts that zero means something for it — wrong for a rate or an index. Fill one series at most: two translucent fills overlap into a third quantity that is not there.

Interactive

interactive makes every reading focusable and clickable, and grows an invisible marker on a series drawn without one so there is something to land on. The hit area is a transparent 24px stroke on a fine pointer and 44px on a coarse one — a 4px dot is not a touch target. Tab to a marker and the tooltip names that series alone; hover the plot and it names every series at the category. A tap latches the tooltip open until the next tap outside. pointClicked fires whether or not the flag is set. dimOthers is a separate opt-in, off by default on all five charts: fading the rest costs the separation between the faded marks, so it buys loudness for one reading and pays for it with the others.

Patterns

patterns gives every series a dash of its own, in palette-slot order, so the lines are told apart by shape as well as by colour — WCAG technique G111. Slot 1 stays solid, so a single-series chart is unchanged by turning it on. Off by default: the shipped palette clears the colour-blind separation threshold on its own, and this is for monochrome print, a retuned palette, or a reader who asks for it.

patterns = false (default)

Filtering legend

legendFilters turns each legend entry into a checkbox that switches its series off, and hiddenSeries is two-way, so a page can drive it from a URL parameter. The value axis is scaled from every series, hidden or not, unless rescaleOnHide says otherwise — a silent rescale turns a period-over-period comparison into a different claim. Hovering an entry dims the other series. Switch them all off and the plot is replaced by an explanation rather than an empty grid.
