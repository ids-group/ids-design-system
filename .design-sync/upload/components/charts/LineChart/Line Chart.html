Series over shared categories, with an optional second y axis for other units.

Default

Series take palette colours in order. slot (1-based) pins a series to a palette slot, so hiding one keeps the others' colours.

Annotations

annotations pins a label over one reading, visible in screenshots and print.

  • Each takes a categoryIndex and an optional seriesId (default: the first plotted series).
  • Edge labels anchor to the edge; labels on hidden series draw nothing.
  • aria-hidden ; their text joins the chart's accessible name.

Area fill

area: true fills to the baseline, implying zero matters - wrong for a rate or index. Fill one series at most: overlaps read as a third quantity.

Pinning the value axis

A derived axis differs per dataset: similar plots can be four times apart.

  • max pins it; null (default) derives it.
  • maxSecondary does the same for axis: 'secondary' .
derived axis · a quiet series fills the plot
max = 5000 · the same data, read against a fixed ceiling

A maximum below the data never clips: the larger wins.

Stagger

  • staggerMs draws series in turn: default 40, 0 all at once, spread within 260ms.
  • Solid lines draw on; dashed lines and areas fade in; markers settle last.

Nothing left to draw

Interactive

  • interactive makes every reading focusable and clickable, with invisible markers where a series has none.
  • Hit area: a transparent 24px stroke on a fine pointer, 44px on a coarse one.
  • The default tooltip="axis" names every series at the category; Tab lands on its band. tooltip="item" : no band, Tab and tooltip address one marker.
  • A press pins the whole category. pointClicked fires with or without interactive .
  • Keys, pin and dimOthers: Charts foundations .

Patterns

Filtering legend

  • legendFilters makes legend entries switches.
  • hiddenSeries is two-way, e.g. for a URL parameter.
  • The axis scales from all series, hidden too, unless rescaleOnHide is set.
  • Resting on an entry fades the others; focusing fades at once.
  • With all hidden, the plot shows an explanation.
