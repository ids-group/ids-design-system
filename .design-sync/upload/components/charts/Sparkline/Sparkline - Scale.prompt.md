# Sparkline - scale

One series, two pictures: service availability over a year, 93.1-99.5 %. data, the default, spans the readings' own range and shows the dip in May. zero keeps zero on the scale and shows the size of it - a sliver at the top. Pick zero where the amount matters more than the movement. valueFormat formats the tooltip value; unset, the locale's number format.

Full API: `Sparkline.d.ts`. How to use the component anywhere: `Sparkline.prompt.md`.
