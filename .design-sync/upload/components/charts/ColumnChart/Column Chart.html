More than one column per category

Named series in data: one column per series per category, with a legend. A flat list is one full-band series.

stack : 'grouped' or the default 'none' sets the columns side by side, 'stacked' stacks them, 'normalized' stacks them as shares of the category total.

  • Hovering or focusing a legend item highlights its series.
  • A series has one colour; a flat list colours by position, its marks being the categories.
  • A series-wide fill applies to all its columns.
  • Segments are split by a 2px surface-colour gap, a second encoding for print and colour-vision deficiency.
  • Under normalized only the axis shows shares; segment values stay readings.
  • legendFilters lets the legend hide a series (legendMode, hiddenSeries ): its columns sink to the baseline, the rest settle and the axis fits what is left.
  • [rescaleOnHide]="false" holds the axis where it stands with every series shown.

Sorting and a range total

  • A column keeps its id across a reorder and moves to its new place.
  • selectRange (off by default): drag across columns for their sum and mean; Escape or a press outside clears it.

Columns and a line, on two scales

No combo component: ds-chart with a dsColumnSeries and a dsLineSeries .

  • axis: 'secondary' uses the second axis, tied by secondaryAxisMode (default 'aligned' : the first axis's gridlines, a round step).
  • Each axis takes its series' colour.
  • Marks paint in source order, so the line comes last.
  • One panel shows both readings.
