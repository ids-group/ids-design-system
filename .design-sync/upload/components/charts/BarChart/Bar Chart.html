Bar chart

Horizontal bars, each a row's value as a share of the maximum.

Default

Rows keep the order given; the chart never sorts. Bar length and percent are shares of max : the input, else the largest bar end, target or band.

Floating bars

A value of [low, high] spans the bar between its two ends (either order), not from zero, to show a duration and its placement (a phase, a shift, a min-to-max).

Target and bands

target draws a tick at one reading (a goal, a budget, last period); ranges paints bands behind the bar, given as upper bounds, in one neutral hue at rising opacity: a bullet chart.

Row colour

Rows share the single-series colour unless one sets color .

Edit data

Segmented rows

segments split a row's bar into parts sized against their own sum, always filling the bar; a sum that differs from value is a data error, not a gap. Parts take the categorical palette in order unless one sets color ; the row's colour is unused. The data table nests them; the tooltip lists each with its share. Parts have no text, so draw a key from --ds-chart-series-N-color in segment order.

Singling out a segment

highlighted names one segment by its id ; every other part in every row fades to --ds-chart-dim-opacity . Screen readers hear nothing; the control that chose it announces it.

A second reading per row

detail prints a second, preformatted reading under the label (the other half of a ratio), and in the data table.

Share of the total

partOfWhole declares the rows add up to a real total (a budget, a headcount) and prints each row's share on the tooltip's second line and as a Share of total column. Off by default: a funnel or time series sums to nothing real. The percent beside each row is the share of max , not of the total.

Pinning a reading

A click holds the row and its tooltip, marked by a pin disc; Unpin appears at the top end, on the caption line. Esc , a second click or a press elsewhere releases it. rowClicked fires either way. pinOnClick="false" turns it off, or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false app-wide.

Edit data

Percent beside the value

showPercent (on by default) prints each row's share of max beside its value. Turn off for narrow rows or alongside partOfWhole . A row's own percent replaces the computed one.

showPercent = true (default)
showPercent = false

Interaction

interactive adds the hover tint and makes the rows one tab stop, switching their role from img to a group of buttons. Arrow keys move between rows (wrapping), Home and End jump to the ends, Enter or Space fires rowClicked. Pointer cursor: on interactive rows and any row a click would hold. rowClicked fires with or without the flag. dimOthers fades all rows but the hovered or focused one, label included; off by default, since faded rows lose separation.

Stagger

staggerMs spaces out the rows' arrivals (default 40 ; the whole spread fits in 260ms; 0 turns it off).

Data table

none (default) renders no table; toggle puts one behind a closed disclosure; visible renders it open.
