Floating bars

A value of [low, high] spans the bar between its ends, in either order: a phase, a shift, a min-to-max.

Target and bands

A tick at one reading: a goal, a budget, last period.

Segmented rows

  • segments split a row's bar into parts sized against their own sum, always filling the bar.
  • A sum that differs from value is a data error, not a gap.
  • Parts take the categorical palette in order unless one sets color ; the row's colour is unused.
  • The data table nests them; the tooltip lists each with its share.
  • Parts have no text: draw a key from --ds-chart-series-N-color in segment order.

Singling out a segment

highlighted names one segment by id ; every other part in every row fades to --ds-chart-dim-opacity.

A second reading per row

detail prints a second, preformatted reading under the label (the other half of a ratio), and in the data table.

Share of the total

  • partOfWhole declares the rows add up to a real total: a budget, a headcount.
  • Each row's share prints on the tooltip's second line and in a Share of total column.
  • Off by default: a funnel or time series sums to nothing real.
  • The percent beside each row is the share of max , not of the total.

Percent beside the value

  • showPercent (default on) prints each row's share of max beside its value.
  • Turn off for narrow rows or alongside partOfWhole .
  • A row's own percent replaces the computed one.
showPercent = true (default)
showPercent = false

Label beside the bar

labelPosition="start" puts each label in a column before its bar; labels, bars, values and percents line up across rows.

  • --ds-bar-chart-label-column-max-width - label column cap, default 40%.
  • --ds-bar-chart-row-gap - var(--space-3) here, var(--space-5) under 'top' .

Bar height

--ds-bar-chart-track-height sets the bar's thickness (default 12px).

Rows inside a row

  • children nests rows under one, behind a disclosure button after its label, closed at first. One level deep.
  • A child's bar and percent are shares of its parent's value, not of max .
  • A child takes the parent's colour and showPercent unless it sets its own.
  • The data table lists every child, open or closed.
  • DsIntl.chart.rowsUnder - disclosure button name.
  • interactive - Right opens a group, Left closes it.

A defined term

hint turns the label into a term: a dotted underline, and the hint in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, announced as the label's description.

Bold rows

emphasis prints a row's label in bold, as for the first and last rows of a funnel.

Percent on some rows

A row's own showPercent overrides the chart's: here the chart hides percents and one group shows them.

A row apart

detached marks a row that is not a step of the others, such as a total who finished or left.

A dashed divider sets it apart; partOfWhole gives it no share.
