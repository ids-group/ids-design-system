Bar - what the bar is

One horizontal track with one fill: a task's completion, or a measurement on a known scale.

The two readings are the same drawing and a different claim, and semantics is which one you are making. 'progress' , the default, is how far a task has got and carries role="progressbar" . 'meter' is a measurement on a known scale — a rate, a score, a count against a ceiling — which has no beginning and no completion, and carries role="meter" . 'none' draws the bar and hides it from assistive technology, for a cell that prints its figure beside it.

This is not cosmetic. A table of two hundred rates left at the default announces two hundred progress bars, and the row already said the number.
