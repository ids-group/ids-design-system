Avatars - seeded tint

A seed gives a deterministic hsl(H 50% 62%) tint with white initials, identical across apps. avatarTint and initialsFrom (also takes one full name) are exported.

  • The tint hashes name, never the initials: Alice Brown and Adam Bell (both "AB") get 198° and 356°.
  • initialsRule is for a script or custom the default rule misses: Spanish wants MG for "María José García López".
