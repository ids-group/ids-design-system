Buttons - full width

Set fullWidth to stretch the button to its container — for mobile primary CTAs and full-bleed form submits. The host becomes block-level at 100% width.

align decides where the contents sit once the button is wider than them, which in practice means beside fullWidth . 'between' pushes the label and a trailing icon to opposite edges, which is what a full-bleed row action wants: the label reads from the left and the chevron sits where the eye leaves.
