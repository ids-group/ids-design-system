Filter bar - sizing the "+N" trigger

<FilterBar />

Priority+ filter row: as many filters as fit, the rest behind "+N more".

triggerSize matches the trigger to the filters beside it — default md . The bar cannot work this out on its own: dsFilterItem carries a WIDTH, and the filters are arbitrary projected controls whose own size it never sees. Left unset beside sm filters, the trigger stands 8px taller than everything next to it, since the row aligns on flex-end .

triggerSize sm md lg 'md' Height of the "+N" trigger, to match the filters beside it. The bar cannot infer this: `dsFilterItem` carries a WIDTH, and the filters are arbitrary projected controls whose own `size` it never sees — so a row of `sm` filters got an `md` trigger standing 8px taller under `align-items: flex-end`.
