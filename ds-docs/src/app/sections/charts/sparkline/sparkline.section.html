A word-sized trend line for a table cell or stat.

Default

One svg, no container to measure: cheap in a thousand-row table. A tooltip shows on hover. Decorative unless it gets a label . With a label and tooltip on (default) it is a tab stop: focus opens the tooltip on the last reading; Left, Right, Home and End move it.

Sized by --ds-sparkline-width / --ds-sparkline-height (80 x 24px); line styled by --ds-sparkline-line-color (series 1), --ds-sparkline-line-width (1.5px) and --ds-sparkline-dot-radius (2.5px).

Scale

data (default) spans the readings' range; zero keeps zero on the scale, for when amount matters more than movement. valueFormat formats the tooltip value; unset, the locale's number format.

Area

area fills down to the baseline: zero on the zero scale, the foot of the box on data . Colour --ds-sparkline-area-color (default: the line's) at --ds-sparkline-area-opacity .

null breaks the line, not zero. [tooltip]="false" makes it inert, for a row that is a link.
