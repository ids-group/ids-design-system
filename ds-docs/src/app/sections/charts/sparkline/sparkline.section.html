A word-sized trend line for a table cell or stat.

Default

  • One svg, no container to measure: cheap in a thousand-row table.
  • A tooltip shows on hover.
  • Decorative unless it gets a label.
  • With a label and tooltip on (default) it is a tab stop: focus opens the tooltip on the last reading; ← → Home End move it.

Scale

The line spans the readings' range; with scale="zero" zero stays on the scale, for when amount matters more than movement.

valueFormat formats the tooltip value; unset, the locale's number format.

scale="data" (default)

Area

  • area fills down to the baseline: zero on the zero scale, the foot of the box on data.
  • Colour --ds-sparkline-area-color (default: the line's) at --ds-sparkline-area-opacity .
