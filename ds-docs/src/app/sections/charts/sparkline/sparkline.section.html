A word-sized trend line for a table cell or a stat: no axes, gridlines, legend or frame, a dot on the last reading and a tooltip on hover.

Default

One svg per instance, with no container to measure, so a table of a thousand rows stays cheap. It is decorative unless it gets a label: beside a value that already says the number, the line only adds the shape. With a label it is also a tab stop: focus opens the tooltip on the last reading, the arrow keys, Home and End move it.

@for (row of rows; track row.name) { }
{{ row.name }} {{ row.value }}

Scale

One series, two pictures: service availability over a year, 93.1-99.5 %. data , the default, spans the readings' own range and shows the dip in May. zero keeps zero on the scale and shows the size of it - a sliver at the top. Pick zero where the amount matters more than the movement.

scale="data" (default)
scale="zero"

Area

area fills the band under the line down to the baseline: zero on the zero scale, the foot of the box on data . Its colour is --ds-sparkline-area-color , the line's own by default, at --ds-sparkline-area-opacity .

area
area, scale="zero"

Gaps and no tooltip

A null breaks the line rather than drawing a zero. [tooltip]="false" leaves the line inert, for a row that is itself a link.
