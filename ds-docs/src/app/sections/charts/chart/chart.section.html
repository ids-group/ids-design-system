Plot, axes, grid, value domain and states, but no marks: the container inside column, line and waterfall charts. Use it when no preset fits.

Composing a chart

  • A mark is an attribute on a real <svg:g> : SVG renders nothing for unknown elements.
  • Without the svg: prefix, Angular rejects a projected SVG element at compile time.
  • Paint order, whatever the written order: reference lines, then marks (in written order), then reference labels.

Category cursor

cursor : 'category' draws a band and crosshair on the hovered category, and the panel lists every reading there; 'none' lets each mark answer alone; 'auto' (default) is 'category' with two or more tooltip-showing marks, or one with tooltip="axis" , else 'none' .

  • The band is the only hover area: each plot point belongs to its nearest category, so bands are contiguous.
  • Marks inside open no panel on hover and do not pin on click.
  • A click on a mark fires pointClicked; a click on the band pins the category.
  • With a dsLineSeries in the chart, a pinned category keeps its line; a column-only chart keeps the pin alone.
  • The axes answer the pointer: a bold category label, a guide and the value in a pill on the axis. showAxisReadout="false" turns it off, on ds-chart and the column, line and waterfall charts.
  • The pill prints through the chart's valueFormat at the location 'tooltip' , in full. A formatter that sets handlesReadout: true is asked for 'readout' there instead, and can return a short form.
  • Keys, with interactive on chart and marks: the category cursor table in Charts foundations .
cursor = auto, one mark: each column answers
cursor = none, two marks: each mark answers
highlightActive = false: no column brightens
showAxisReadout = false: no label, guide or pill
valueFormat: a compact pill, a full tooltip

Two value axes

A mark picks its axis: axis: 'secondary' in line data, axis="secondary" on a column series; secondaryAxisMode relates it to the first:

  • 'aligned' (default) - on the first axis's gridlines at a round step; one plot.
  • 'split' - two plots over the same category axis.
  • 'exact' - one grid; the second axis ends at its largest reading, with unround labels.
  • 'dual-grid' - each axis scaled and gridded on its own.

axisColor and secondaryAxisColor colour each axis's labels like its marks - the only cue to which axis a mark reads.

Either mark takes either side: here columns read the right axis.

Cumulative line over its columns: Column chart , "Columns and a line, on two scales".

Annotations

dsSeriesAnnotation pins a label to a reading, legible without a pointer and in print.

  • Pass the reading: categoryIndex, value , optional axis . Marks never see each other.
  • It reports no domain.

Naming the axes, and holding the cursor

axisLabel / secondaryAxisLabel
Each axis's unit, rotated down its gutter.
maxSecondary
Pins the second axis's ceiling as max pins the first, so compared plots do not rescale.
rescaleOnHide
On by default: a hidden series leaves the axes. Off, its values still hold them, so hiding a series moves no gridline.
  • A press holds the category cursor: a pin disc at the band's foot, Unpin on the caption line, atop the plot without one.
  • Release and opt-out: Charts foundations .
  • cursorPinned() / releaseCursor() read and release the pin from code.
  • Without the cursor, hovering an axis label acts as hovering its column.

The tooltip of a composed chart

  • The container opens the one tooltip; bind nothing on marks.
  • tooltipTemplate replaces the panel body; it receives ChartTooltipContext, values formatted by the chart's valueFormat .
  • tooltipExtraGap moves the panel off the mark, clearing printed value labels.
  • A line sits on a surface-colour band, outline (default on), legible across columns.
