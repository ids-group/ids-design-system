The container the column, line and waterfall charts are built inside. It owns the plot, the axes, the grid, the value domain and the loading, empty and error states, and draws no marks of its own - reach for it directly when no preset offers the combination you need.

Composing a chart

A mark is an ATTRIBUTE on a real <svg:g> - dsColumnSeries , dsLineSeries , dsReferenceLine , dsReferenceLabel - because an element the SVG specification does not know renders nothing and neither do its children. The svg: prefix is Angular's, and a projected SVG element without it is rejected at compile time.

The container has three slots and they are layers rather than an ordering preference: a reference line paints UNDER the marks whatever order you wrote it in, the default slot takes the marks, and a reference label paints OVER them, so an annotation's stroke stays furniture and its name survives a column drawn through it. Inside the default slot the marks paint in the order they are written.

This one is a column series with a target band behind it and the band's names over it. ds-column-chart wraps exactly this arrangement behind its own thresholds input; written out, that input is what it builds.

Mark inputs

dsColumnSeries

  • data - the points, one per category.
  • showValues - prints each value above its column (default on).
  • interactive - pointer cursor and hover tint; pointClicked fires either way.
  • pinOnClick - holds the clicked mark; unset falls back to DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick, then true.
  • dimOthers - fades every mark but the one under the pointer.
  • staggerMs - delay between marks' entrances (default 40, 0 off).
  • partOfWhole - adds each mark's share to the tooltip.
  • tooltip - whether a hovered or focused column opens a tooltip.
  • tooltipAnchor - 'mark' (default) or 'cell', the full-height category cell.
  • tooltipLingerMs - how long the panel survives the pointer leaving.
  • valueFormat - formats every value the series prints.
  • columnWidthRatio - the mark's share of its slot before the maximum width caps it (default 0.8).
  • slot / slotCount - this series' position among the slots a category is divided into, and how many; grouped columns by hand.
  • offsets - where each mark starts and ends on the value axis; stacked columns by hand. Unset, marks run from zero.
  • soleSeries - the only series in its slot; each mark answers over the whole plot height.
  • axis - 'primary' (default) or 'secondary'.
  • seriesColor / seriesFill - one colour or fill ('solid' / 'hatched') for the series; a point's own wins.
  • seriesLabel - names the series in the tooltip.
  • seriesKey / highlighted - identifies the series to a legend; every series but the highlighted one fades.
  • pointClicked - output: the clicked point, on click, Enter or Space.

dsLineSeries

  • data - the series; a null reading is missing. Each entry takes axis, area, lower and color.
  • showMarkers - a marker at every reading (default on).
  • outline - a background-colour band under the line (default on).
  • interactive , pinOnClick, dimOthers, staggerMs, tooltipLingerMs, valueFormat - as on a column series.
  • highlighted - the series to single out, whatever dimOthers says.
  • patterns - a dash pattern per series, in series order.
  • stacked - fills each area from the series below it.
  • gaps - what a null draws: 'break' (default) or 'zero'.
  • curve - 'linear' (default), 'step' or 'smooth'.
  • tooltip - 'item' (default) names the marker, 'axis' every series at the category.
  • tooltipAnchor - where an 'axis' panel hangs: 'reading' (default) or 'pointer'.
  • pointClicked - output: the reading, its series and its category position.

dsReferenceLine / dsReferenceLabel

  • thresholds - the same ChartThreshold list on both: value, optional label, color (default --ds-chart-threshold-color), dash ('solid' / 'dashed', the default, / 'dotted') and to, which makes it a band. Each widens the value axis.

dsSeriesAnnotation

  • annotations - ChartAnnotation entries: categoryIndex (0-based), value, label, optional axis.

Category cursor

cursor="category" draws a band and crosshair down the category under the pointer and reports it to every mark. The default 'auto' turns it on for two or more tooltip-showing marks, or a mark with tooltip="axis" ; the panel then names every reading at that category. A single mark otherwise answers per mark.

Under the cursor the category's band is the one hover area: the pointer anywhere in it opens the band and the tooltip together, on a plot of one mark as on a composed one, and every point of the plot belongs to its nearest category, so the pointer moves from one band straight to the next. A mark inside the band opens no panel of its own on hover and does not pin on click. With interactive set on the chart and on its marks, Tab lands on the category's band, ringed, and opens its panel naming every mark's reading there; ← and → walk the bands and wrap, Home and End jump to the ends, Tab again leaves the chart, and ↑ and ↓ focus one mark's reading, whose panel names it alone. A click on a mark, or Enter on a focused one, still fires pointClicked , and with pinOnClick a click on the band holds the category; so does Enter or Space on a focused band, until the focus moves to another band. Esc steps back from a mark to its band, and on the band closes the panel.

Two value axes

A mark declares which axis it is measured against - axis: 'secondary' in a line series' data, axis="secondary" on a column series - and secondaryAxisMode decides how that axis relates to the first. 'aligned' , the default, hangs it on the first axis's own gridlines at a round step, so both marks share one plot; 'split' draws two plots over the same category axis; 'exact' keeps one set of gridlines but ends the second axis at its largest reading, so its labels are not round; 'dual-grid' scales and grids each axis on its own. axisColor and secondaryAxisColor paint each axis's labels in the colour of the marks read against it, which is the only thing on the canvas that says which numbers a mark answers to.

Either mark can take either side. Here the columns read the right-hand axis and the line the left, which is the arrangement to keep when the screen being replaced put them that way round.

The dressed single-plot case, a cumulative line over the columns it sums, is on the column chart page under "Columns and a line, on two scales".

Annotations

dsSeriesAnnotation pins a label to one reading so it reads without a pointer, in print or a screenshot. The caller passes the reading (categoryIndex, value , optional axis ), since a mark never sees the other marks; it reports no domain.

The annex table

ds-chart-data-table is the annex every chart puts under its plot: each reading in a details a reader can open. Cells arrive already formatted, because how a value reads is the chart's decision and not the table's. rowHeader renders each row's first cell as a th scope="row" , for a matrix whose rows are named rather than numbered; nested on a row draws it as a part of the row above; and toggled fires when the disclosure changes - which is how the scatter chart builds twelve thousand rows only once they are asked for.

Naming the axes, and holding the cursor

axisLabel and secondaryAxisLabel name the unit each set of ticks counts, rotated down its own gutter. maxSecondary pins the second axis's ceiling the way max pins the first, so two plots read against each other do not rescale under one another. A press holds the category cursor where it is, by default, with a pin disc at the foot of its band: press again, Escape, or a press outside the plot to let go. Set pinOnClick="false" to keep it moving with the pointer. While the cursor is held the chart draws an Unpin pill on its caption's line, or without a caption over the top end of the plot, as the preset charts do; cursorPinned() and releaseCursor() read and release the pin from code of your own.

Without the category cursor, the pointer on a category's label on the axis answers for its column exactly as the column does: its tooltip opens, and the rest dim only under dimOthers .

The tooltip of a composed chart

The container opens the one tooltip its marks describe: a mark hands it what the panel shows, so nothing is bound on the mark itself. tooltipTemplate replaces the panel's body and receives the ChartTooltipContext - the category and one entry per series, each already through the chart's valueFormat . tooltipExtraGap pushes the panel further from the mark, for a plot that prints value labels in the gap it would take.

A line series sits on a band of the surface colour - outline , on by default - so it stays legible where it crosses the columns; switch it off here to compare.

What a line series does on its own

These are the mark's inputs, not the preset's - reach for them once ds-line-chart stops fitting. highlighted names the one series to single out and fades the rest whatever dimOthers says - dimOthers governs only the fade a pointer over a marker starts. The singled-out line thickens as well as keeping its colour, so the comparison survives a monochrome print. patterns gives each series a dash of its own, curve takes 'linear' / 'step' / 'smooth' , stacked separates a piled band from the one under it, pinOnClick holds a reading on a plot without the category cursor (under the cursor the container's pinOnClick holds the whole category instead), and tooltipAnchor decides whether an 'axis' panel hangs over the highest reading or follows the pointer. A series entry's area takes true for a flat tint or 'fade' for a tint that fades out at the baseline, as on the line chart page under "Area fill".
