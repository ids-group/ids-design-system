The container inside the column, line and waterfall charts: plot, axes, grid, value domain and loading, empty and error states, but no marks. Use it directly when no preset fits.

Composing a chart

A mark is an ATTRIBUTE on a real <svg:g> - dsColumnSeries , dsLineSeries , dsReferenceLine , dsReferenceLabel - since SVG renders nothing for unknown elements or their children. Angular rejects a projected SVG element without the svg: prefix at compile time.

Three slots, layered regardless of order: reference lines paint UNDER the marks, the default slot holds marks (in written order), reference labels paint OVER them.

ds-column-chart builds this demo from its thresholds input.

Mark inputs

dsColumnSeries

  • data - one point per category.
  • showValues - values above columns (default on).
  • interactive - pointer cursor and hover tint; pointClicked fires either way.
  • pinOnClick - holds the clicked mark; unset falls back to DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick, then true.
  • dimOthers - fades all but the hovered mark.
  • staggerMs - entrance delay between marks (default 40, 0 off).
  • partOfWhole - share in the tooltip.
  • tooltip - whether hover or focus opens a tooltip.
  • tooltipAnchor - 'mark' (default) or 'cell', the full-height category cell.
  • tooltipLingerMs - how long the panel outlives the pointer.
  • valueFormat - formats printed values.
  • columnWidthRatio - share of the slot before the max-width cap (default 0.8).
  • slot / slotCount - this series' slot in a category, and how many; hand-grouped columns.
  • offsets - each mark's value-axis start and end; hand-stacked columns. Unset, from zero.
  • soleSeries - alone in its slot; marks answer over the full plot height.
  • axis - 'primary' (default) or 'secondary'.
  • seriesColor / seriesFill - series colour or fill ('solid' / 'hatched'); a point's own wins.
  • seriesLabel - names the series in the tooltip.
  • seriesKey / highlighted - legend identity; all but the highlighted series fade.
  • pointClicked - output: the point, on click, Enter or Space.

dsLineSeries

  • data - the series; null means missing. Entries take axis, area, lower and color.
  • showMarkers - marker per reading (default on).
  • outline - background-colour band under the line (default on).
  • interactive , pinOnClick, dimOthers, staggerMs, tooltipLingerMs, valueFormat - as on a column series.
  • highlighted - singles out a series, whatever dimOthers says.
  • patterns - dash pattern per series, in order.
  • stacked - fills each area from the series below it.
  • gaps - what a null draws: 'break' (default) or 'zero'.
  • curve - 'linear' (default), 'step' or 'smooth'.
  • tooltip - 'item' (default) names the marker, 'axis' every series at the category.
  • tooltipAnchor - where an 'axis' panel hangs: 'reading' (default, over the highest) or 'pointer'.
  • pointClicked - output: reading, series and category position.

dsReferenceLine / dsReferenceLabel

  • thresholds - one ChartThreshold list for both: value, optional label, color (default --ds-chart-threshold-color), dash ('solid' / 'dashed', the default, / 'dotted') and to for a band. Each widens the value axis.

dsSeriesAnnotation

  • annotations - ChartAnnotation entries: categoryIndex (0-based), value, label, optional axis.

Category cursor

cursor="category" draws a band and crosshair on the hovered category and reports it to every mark. Default 'auto' enables it for two or more tooltip-showing marks, or one with tooltip="axis" ; the panel then lists every reading there. Otherwise each mark answers alone.

Under the cursor the band is the only hover area: anywhere in it opens band and tooltip. Every plot point belongs to its nearest category, so bands are contiguous. Marks inside open no panel on hover and do not pin on click. With interactive on chart and marks:

  • Tab rings the band and opens its panel of all readings; Tab again leaves.
  • ← / → walk the bands and wrap; Home / End jump to the ends.
  • ↑ / ↓ focus one mark's reading, whose panel names it alone; Esc steps back to the band, and on the band closes the panel.
  • A click on a mark, or Enter on a focused one, fires pointClicked .
  • With pinOnClick , a click on the band, or Enter / Space on a focused band, holds the category until focus moves to another band.

Two value axes

A mark picks its axis: axis: 'secondary' in line data, axis="secondary" on a column series; secondaryAxisMode relates it to the first:

  • 'aligned' (default) - on the first axis's gridlines at a round step; one plot.
  • 'split' - two plots over the same category axis.
  • 'exact' - one grid; the second axis ends at its largest reading, with unround labels.
  • 'dual-grid' - each axis scaled and gridded on its own.

axisColor and secondaryAxisColor colour each axis's labels like its marks - the only cue to which axis a mark reads.

Either mark takes either side; here columns read the right axis - keep that when the replaced screen did.

Cumulative line over its columns: column chart, "Columns and a line, on two scales".

Annotations

dsSeriesAnnotation pins a label to a reading, legible without a pointer or in print. Pass the reading (categoryIndex, value , optional axis ): marks never see each other. It reports no domain.

The annex table

ds-chart-data-table is every chart's annex: each reading in an openable details . Cells arrive formatted by the chart. rowHeader makes each row's first cell a th scope="row" , for named rows; nested makes a row part of the one above; toggled fires on disclosure change - the scatter chart builds its twelve thousand rows only then.

Naming the axes, and holding the cursor

axisLabel and secondaryAxisLabel name each axis's unit, rotated down its gutter. maxSecondary pins the second axis's ceiling as max pins the first, so compared plots do not rescale. A press holds the category cursor, with a pin disc at the band's foot; press again, Escape or a press outside releases. pinOnClick="false" keeps it following the pointer. While held, an Unpin pill sits on the caption line, or atop the plot without one; cursorPinned() and releaseCursor() read and release the pin from your code.

Without the cursor, hovering an axis label acts as hovering its column: the tooltip opens; the rest dim only under dimOthers .

The tooltip of a composed chart

The container opens the one tooltip; bind nothing on marks. tooltipTemplate replaces the panel body and receives ChartTooltipContext : the category and an entry per series, formatted by the chart's valueFormat . tooltipExtraGap moves the panel off the mark, clearing printed value labels.
