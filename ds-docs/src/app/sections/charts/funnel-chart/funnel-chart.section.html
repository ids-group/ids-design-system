What the Drop column reports

drop chooses the unit. relative , the default, is the share that leaked at this stage; absolute is the count that leaked; none takes the column out. The two units answer different questions — a share is a talking point, a count is a re-targeting job — and the count is only recoverable by subtracting two rows, which a reader should not have to do.

Interaction

interactive turns on the hover tint and makes each band a tab stop with Enter and Space activating it. The pointer cursor shows wherever a click does something: on an interactive band, and on any band a click would hold. stageClicked fires whether or not the flag is set, so a chart that only needs the output does not have to advertise the affordance. Hovering a row of the stage table reads as hovering the band it names: once the pointer has rested, the same tooltip opens over the BAND, and the rest of the funnel dims with it whatever dimOthers says.

Holding a band

A click holds the band and its tooltip in place, so the reading stays while you look away from the chart. A pin disc marks the held band, and an Unpin control appears at the top end of the chart, on the caption's line. Esc releases it, and so does clicking the band again or a press elsewhere. With dimOthers the rest of the funnel stays down too, which is what keeps the comparison on screen after the pointer has gone. Set pinOnClick="false" to keep bands unheld, or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for every chart in the app.

default
dimOthers

Stagger

staggerMs spaces the bands' arrivals out as they rise in. 60 is the default, the whole spread fits inside --ds-motion-chart-mark-stagger-budget , and 0 has every band arrive together.
