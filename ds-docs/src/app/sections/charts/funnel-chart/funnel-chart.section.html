The stages of a journey, drawn top to bottom in the order you give them — never sorted, because the order is the meaning.

Default

The default area shape gives each stage a band whose area is proportional to its value, so a small stage buys its area back in depth rather than shrinking to a sliver.

Shape · taper

A fixed silhouette: editing the data moves the table under it and never the shape, because taper encodes nothing in width. Reach for it only when the numbers are read from the table.

Shape · width

Every boundary is read off the values as a fraction of the largest stage, so a stage that has lost nearly everything collapses to a sliver with no room for its label.

Shape · area

The default. The silhouette of taper with the boundaries moved so each band's area is proportional to its value.

What the Drop column reports

drop chooses the unit: relative (default) is the share that leaked at this stage, absolute the count that leaked, none removes the column. Use absolute when the reader needs the count, which is otherwise only recoverable by subtracting two rows.

drop = relative (default)
drop = absolute
drop = none

Interaction

interactive turns on the hover tint and makes the bands one tab stop: arrow keys move between them (wrapping), Home and End jump to the ends, Enter or Space activates. The pointer cursor shows on an interactive band and on any band a click would hold. stageClicked fires whether or not interactive is set. Hovering a stage-table row tints it, opens the same tooltip over its band and dims the rest of the funnel, whatever dimOthers says.

Holding a band

A click holds the band and its tooltip; a pin disc marks it and an Unpin control appears on the caption's line. Esc , clicking the band again or a press elsewhere releases it. With dimOthers the rest stays dimmed while held. pinOnClick="false" turns holding off for one chart, DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for the app.

dimOthers

Stagger

staggerMs spaces the bands' arrivals out as they rise in. 60 is the default, the whole spread fits inside 260ms, and 0 has every band arrive together.

staggerMs = 0
staggerMs = 60 (default)
