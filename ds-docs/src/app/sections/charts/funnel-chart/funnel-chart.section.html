Interaction

interactive adds the hover tint and makes the bands one tab stop: arrows move (wrapping), Home / End jump to the ends, Enter or Space activates. Any band a click would activate or hold shows the pointer. stageClicked fires regardless of interactive . Hovering a stage-table row tints it, shows its band's tooltip and dims the rest, regardless of dimOthers .

Holding a band

A click holds band and tooltip (pin disc; Unpin control on the caption line). Esc , a second click or a press elsewhere releases it. With dimOthers the rest stays dimmed while held. pinOnClick="false" disables holding for one chart, DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for the app.

Stagger

staggerMs staggers the bands' rise-in. Default 60; the spread fits inside 260ms; 0 brings all bands in together.
