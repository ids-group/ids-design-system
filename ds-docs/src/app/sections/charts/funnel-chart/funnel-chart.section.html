The stages of a journey, drawn top to bottom in the order you give them — never sorted, because the order is the meaning.

Default

The default area shape gives each stage a band whose area is proportional to its value, so a small stage buys its area back in depth rather than shrinking to a sliver.

Shape · taper

What the Drop column reports

drop chooses the unit. relative , the default, is the share that leaked at this stage; absolute is the count that leaked; none takes the column out. The two units answer different questions — a share is a talking point, a count is a re-targeting job — and the count is only recoverable by subtracting two rows, which a reader should not have to do.

Interaction

interactive turns on the pointer cursor and the hover tint, and makes each band a tab stop with Enter and Space activating it. stageClicked fires whether or not the flag is set, so a chart that only needs the output does not have to advertise the affordance. Hovering a row of the stage table reads as hovering the band it names: the same tooltip opens over the BAND, and the rest of the funnel dims with it.

Holding a band

pinOnClick holds the clicked band and its tooltip in place, so the reading stays while you look away from the chart. A pin glyph marks the held band and a Reset appears in the corner; Esc releases it, and so does clicking the band again. With dimOthers the rest of the funnel stays down too, which is what keeps the comparison on screen after the pointer has gone.

Stagger

staggerMs spaces the bands' arrivals out rather than fading them all at once. 0 , the default, means no stagger.
