The stages of a journey, drawn top to bottom in the order you give them — they are never sorted, because the order is the meaning. Under the funnel an aligned table repeats each stage's value, its share of the first stage, and how much fell away since the stage above it.

Default

Out of the box the silhouette is decoration: the default taper shape gives every stage the same depth whatever its value, so everything quantitative comes from the table under it. The two shapes below hand that job back to the geometry.

Shape · taper (default)

Width encodes nothing here. The silhouette is fixed — full width at the top, a constant tail at the bottom — and every stage is given the same depth however large or small its value is. Read the numbers from the table, never from how wide a band looks. Switch the shape to width or to area when you need the geometry itself to carry the value.

Shape · width

The fixed silhouette is abandoned and every boundary is read straight off the values, as a fraction of the largest stage. Band widths become directly comparable, and a stage that has lost nearly everything collapses to a sliver with no room for its own label.

Shape · area

The same fixed silhouette as taper, with the stage boundaries moved to the depths that make each band's area proportional to its value. Size still reads as quantity, but nothing can disappear: a small stage buys its area back in depth instead of shrinking to nothing sideways.

Steep drop

A thousand down to ten, under both of the value-carrying shapes. Width is the more literal picture and the less usable one; area spends depth to keep the tail labelled. Choose by what the reader is meant to take away — the size of the collapse, or the stages that survive it.

width — the tail collapses
area — the tail stays labelled

Stage table

The aligned table under the funnel is on by default, and under taper it is the only place the values exist. Turn showTable off for a tile whose numbers already sit beside it — and check first that the shape you chose is one that carries them.

Data table

The disclosure appears only where the stage table is off — with both on, the same numbers would print twice.

Edge cases

Every share is measured against the first stage, so a first stage of zero leaves the percentage column with nothing to divide by. A funnel of one stage is still drawn rather than refused: it is what a filtered report looks like before the filter is loosened.
