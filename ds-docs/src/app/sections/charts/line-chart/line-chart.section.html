Series take palette colours in order. slot (1-based) pins a series to a palette slot, so hiding one keeps the others' colours.

Annotations

annotations pins a label over one reading, visible in screenshots and print. Each takes a categoryIndex and an optional seriesId (default: first plotted series). Edge labels anchor to the edge; labels on hidden series draw nothing. They are aria-hidden; their text joins the chart's accessible name.

Composition over time

A stack shows what the total is made of. stack : stacked starts each series where the one below ended; normalized divides by the category's total, so the axis reads in shares. Only a series with area fills; lines sit on the running total. Stacking is per axis.

Missing readings

A null reading is a hole, not a zero; gaps sets what it draws: break (default) stops the line and resumes after; zero draws through the baseline. Any non-finite value, such as NaN , is treated the same.

Second axis · aligned (default)

A series with axis: 'secondary' uses the right scale; yAxisMode reconciles them. aligned (default) shares gridlines and rounds the secondary step up, so that line stops short of the top.

Second axis · exact

The secondary step comes from the peak: full height, but labels are not round numbers.

Second axis · dual-grid

Each axis is niced on its own and both use the full height, with two sets of gridlines - the secondary's dashed.

Second axis · split

Two plots share a category axis, labelled under the lower one: nothing overlaps, but the shapes cannot be read against each other.

Pinning the value axis

A derived axis differs per dataset, so similar plots can be four times apart. max pins it; null (default) derives it. maxSecondary does the same for axis: 'secondary' .

Stagger

staggerMs draws series in turn (default 40 , 0 = all at once), within 260ms. Solid lines draw on; dashed lines and areas fade in; markers settle last.

Nothing left to draw

When the legend hides every series, noVisibleSeriesText shows at the plot's height. Skeleton, empty and error boxes take --ds-line-chart-placeholder-height when set, a height that includes the axis labels; the plot reads only --ds-line-chart-plot-height .

Data table

none (default) renders no table at all; toggle, a closed disclosure; visible , an open one. The table is the non-visual route, so use none only when the page already tabulates the numbers.

Interactive

interactive makes every reading focusable and clickable (invisible markers where a series has none); the hit area is a transparent 24px stroke on a fine pointer, 44px on a coarse one. The tooltip names every series at the category; with tooltip="item" there is no band and Tab and tooltip address one marker. pointClicked fires with or without interactive. dimOthers is a separate opt-in, off by default.

With interactive and the default tooltip="axis" , Tab lands on a category's band, ringed, panel open (no tab stop otherwise). ← / → move between bands and wrap, Home / End jump to the ends, Tab leaves the chart. Enter or Space on a band pins it (unless pinOnClick is off); moving to another band releases the pin. ↑ / ↓ focus one marker, named alone in the panel; Enter on it fires pointClicked . Esc steps back to the band, then closes the panel.

A press pins the category (band, panel, pin disc) with an Unpin control on the caption's line. A second press on the same category, Esc or a press outside releases it. A focused marker does not pin: Enter / Space fire pointClicked only. Set pinOnClick="false" to keep the cursor following the pointer, or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false app-wide.

tooltipAnchor places the panel: 'reading' (default) holds it still over the category's highest reading; 'pointer' follows the cursor.

What a legend control does

legendMode : isolate (default) shows one series, as radios with All and None. toggle hides one, as checkboxes - to remove a dominant series so the rest read at a usable scale.

Filtering legend

legendFilters makes legend entries switches (radios, or checkboxes with legendMode="toggle" ); hiddenSeries is two-way, e.g. for a URL parameter. The axis scales from all series, hidden too, unless rescaleOnHide is set. Resting on an entry fades the others, focusing fades at once, regardless of dimOthers . With all hidden, the plot shows an explanation.
