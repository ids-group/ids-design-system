Series over shared categories, with an optional second y axis for other units.

Default

Series take palette colours in order. slot (1-based) pins a series to a palette slot, so hiding one keeps the others' colours.

Annotations

annotations pins a label over one reading, visible in screenshots and print.

  • Each takes a categoryIndex and an optional seriesId (default: the first plotted series).
  • Edge labels anchor to the edge; labels on hidden series draw nothing.
  • aria-hidden ; their text joins the chart's accessible name.

Composition over time

stack : by default each series draws from zero; 'stacked' starts each where the one below ended; 'normalized' divides by the category's total, and the axis reads in shares.

Only a series with area fills; lines sit on the running total. Stacking is per axis.

Between the readings

  • 'linear' (default) claims nothing between the readings.
  • 'step' holds each reading until the next: prices, headcounts, rates.
  • 'smooth' is a monotone cubic: no invented peaks or troughs.

Fills follow the curve.

Missing readings

A null or other non-finite reading (NaN) is a hole, not a zero.

gaps : the default 'break' stops the line and resumes after the hole; 'zero' draws through the baseline.

Where two lines cross is an artefact

Second axis · aligned (default)

axis: 'secondary' puts a series on the right scale; yAxisMode reconciles the two.

aligned (default) shares gridlines and rounds the secondary step up, so that line stops short of the top.

Second axis · exact

The secondary step comes from the peak: full height, but labels are not round numbers.

Second axis · dual-grid

Each axis is niced on its own and both use the full height, with two sets of gridlines - the secondary's dashed.

Second axis · split

Two plots share a category axis, labelled under the lower one: nothing overlaps, but the shapes cannot be read against each other.

Markers

Turn showMarkers off when dense markers merge into a band.

Change on hover

showChange thickens the stretches into and out of the hovered reading, each in the rising or falling colour with its change in percent.

Outline

outline (default on) backs lines and markers with a surface-colour band so crossings stay unbroken. Turn off without gridlines or crossings.

Area fill

area: true fills to the baseline, implying zero matters - wrong for a rate or index. Fill one series at most: overlaps read as a third quantity.

area: true
area: 'fade'

Fill opacity is --ds-chart-area-opacity ; a fade starts at --ds-chart-area-fade-opacity by the line. --ds-chart-area-color on the chart overrides every fill's colour.

Long series

Colliding labels rotate, then thin to every nth by measured width; the line keeps every reading.

Tick count

tickCount requests roughly that many gridlines, rounded to a readable step: five over 0–2,740 give 0/1K/2K/3K.

Pinning the value axis

A derived axis differs per dataset: similar plots can be four times apart.

  • max pins it; null (default) derives it.
  • maxSecondary does the same for axis: 'secondary' .
derived axis · a quiet series fills the plot

Stagger

  • staggerMs draws series in turn: default 40, 0 all at once, spread within 260ms.
  • Solid lines draw on; dashed lines and areas fade in; markers settle last.

Nothing left to draw

  • Legend hides every series: noVisibleSeriesText at the plot's height.
  • Skeleton, empty and error boxes take --ds-line-chart-placeholder-height when set, axis labels included.
  • The plot reads only --ds-line-chart-plot-height .

Interactive

  • interactive makes every reading focusable and clickable, with invisible markers where a series has none.
  • Hit area: a transparent 24px stroke on a fine pointer, 44px on a coarse one.
  • The default tooltip="axis" names every series at the category; Tab lands on its band. tooltip="item" : no band, Tab and tooltip address one marker.
  • A press pins the whole category. pointClicked fires with or without interactive .
  • Keys, pin and dimOthers: Charts foundations .

An 'axis' panel stands still over the category's highest reading; with tooltipAnchor="pointer" it follows the cursor.

Patterns

  • patterns gives each series a dash in palette-slot order, separating lines by shape too (WCAG G111); slot 1 stays solid.
  • Off by default: the palette passes colour-blind separation. Use for monochrome print, a retuned palette, or on request.

What a legend control does

  • 'isolate' (default): radios with All and None, one series shown. Read one series at a time.
  • 'toggle' : checkboxes, each hides its series. Remove a dominant series so the rest read at a usable scale.
legendMode = isolate (default), dimOthers
legendMode = toggle

Filtering legend

  • legendFilters makes legend entries switches.
  • hiddenSeries is two-way, e.g. for a URL parameter.
  • The axis re-scales to the series left showing; [rescaleOnHide]="false" holds it where it was.
  • Resting on an entry fades the others; focusing fades at once.
  • With all hidden, the plot shows an explanation.
rescaleOnHide = true (default)
rescaleOnHide = false
