One or more series plotted over a shared set of categories. A series can be measured against a second y axis when it is in different units, a missing reading can break the line or sit at zero, and the category labels thin out rather than crowd once the series gets long.

Default

Series share one set of categories and take palette colours in the order you pass them. The specimen mixes one filled series with one plain, which is how you say that the filled one is the context the other is read against rather than its equal.

Missing readings

A null reading is a hole, not a zero, and gaps decides what that hole looks like. break stops the line and picks it up after; zero draws through the baseline and is honest only where a missing reading really does mean nothing happened. Anything that is not a finite number takes the same route, so a NaN from a division upstream lands here too.

Second axis · rounded (default)

A series in other units sets axis: 'secondary' and is measured against the right-hand scale; yAxisMode decides how the two scales are reconciled. Rounded keeps one set of gridlines for both and rounds the secondary step up, so against a peak of 75 the right-hand axis labels 0·20·40·60·80·100 and the conversion line stops short of the top.

Second axis · exact

The same gridlines and the same data, with the secondary step taken straight from the peak: 0·15·30·45·60·75. The conversion line now uses the full height, and the labels are no longer round numbers. Pick it when the shape of the secondary series matters more than reading values off its axis.

Second axis · independent

Neither axis is forced through the other's intervals: each is niced on its own and both series use the full height. The cost is two sets of gridlines in one plot, so the secondary's are dashed — without that the reader has no way to tell which lines the right-hand labels belong to.

Second axis · stacked

Two plots one above the other over a shared category axis, labelled under the lower plot only. Nothing overlaps, which makes it the safe mode when the two series would otherwise cross repeatedly — at the price of the one thing a dual axis is usually for, reading the two shapes against each other at a glance.

Markers

A marker per reading says where the data actually is, as opposed to where the line interpolates. Turn showMarkers off once the readings are dense enough that the markers merge into a band, which is roughly where the category labels start thinning.

Area fill

area: true fills the series down to its axis baseline, which asserts that zero means something for it — wrong for a rate or an index. Fill one series at most: two translucent fills overlap, and the overlap reads as a third quantity that is not there.

Long series

At most twelve category labels are drawn, whatever the length: the chart keeps every nth and drops the rest, because every label at thirty-six days is a grey smear that says less than none. The line itself still carries every reading.

Data table

The plot is one image with a generated description, so the annex table is not what makes the chart readable — it is what makes the figures selectable. toggle , the default, leaves the disclosure closed; visible renders it open; none leaves it out.

Edge cases

Loading swaps the plot for a skeleton at the height the plot will be, so the page does not jump when the numbers land. Empty cannot do the same: the axes are derived from the series, so with nothing to scale there is nothing to draw and the chart falls back to its emptyText line.
