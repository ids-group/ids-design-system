Two continuous quantities against each other - weight against carriage cost, distance against time, volume against conversion. The only chart in the family whose mark count the caller decides, and the only one that thins its own data before drawing.

Default

Both scales are continuous: a point carries its own x and y, not a category index. Set xLabel and yLabel ; without them the reader has two numbers and no idea which is which. Marks are semi-transparent (--ds-scatter-chart-dot-opacity, 0.72; radius --ds-scatter-chart-dot-radius) so a pile-up of forty reads darker than four, and they settle in together; a scatter has no stagger.

tickCount is a hint for both axes; ticks snap to a round step. dimOthers fades every mark but the one under the pointer, and tooltip="none" drops the panel.

Several series

Each series takes its own palette slot, or its own color , and the legend keys them. Two clouds that overlap are the reading, not a defect - it is the case the transparency exists for.

Thinning, and saying so

SVG cost scales with nodes, not readings: a line is one <path> , a scatter one node per point (Lighthouse warns near 800, errors near 1400). Past four times the plot's width in marks a series is decimated, and the chart prints how many readings it dropped. With dataTable set, the table still lists every reading.

decimator picks how. minMax , the default, keeps each bucket's highest and lowest reading by construction, so the spike a reader is looking for cannot be the point that was dropped. lttb draws a truer silhouette at the same budget and makes no such promise. none is the caller taking the node budget on themselves.

decimator = minMax (default)
decimator = lttb

Drilling in

interactive adds the pointer affordance and makes each mark a focusable button. pointClicked fires on every click, with or without it, with the series and the reading.

Holding a point

A click on a point holds it and its tooltip in place, with a pin disc over it and an Unpin control at the top end of the chart. A second click, Esc or a press elsewhere lets go, and pointClicked fires either way. Enter and Space on a point fire pointClicked and do not pin. Set pinOnClick="false" to keep points unheld, or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for every chart.
