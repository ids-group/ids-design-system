Two continuous quantities against each other, such as weight against carriage cost. The caller decides the mark count, and the chart thins its own data before drawing.

Default

Both scales are continuous: a point carries its own x and y. Always set xLabel and yLabel . Marks are semi-transparent (--ds-scatter-chart-dot-opacity, 0.72; radius --ds-scatter-chart-dot-radius) so pile-ups read darker, and enter together with no stagger.

tickCount hints both axes; ticks snap to a round step. dimOthers fades all marks but the hovered one; tooltip="none" drops the panel.

Several series

Each series takes its own palette slot, or its own color ; the legend keys them. Overlapping clouds are the reading, not a defect.

Thinning, and saying so

A scatter costs one SVG node per point, unlike one <path> per line (Lighthouse warns near 800, errors near 1400). Past four times the plot width in marks, a series is decimated and the chart prints how many readings it dropped. dataTable still lists every reading.

decimator picks how. minMax (default) keeps each bucket's highest and lowest reading, so spikes are never dropped. lttb draws a truer silhouette without that promise. none leaves the node budget to the caller.

decimator = minMax (default)
decimator = lttb

Drilling in

interactive adds the pointer affordance and makes marks focusable buttons. pointClicked fires on every click, with or without interactive, with series and reading.

Holding a point

A click pins the point and its tooltip, with a pin disc and an Unpin control at the chart's top end. A second click, Esc or a press elsewhere releases; a click fires pointClicked whether it pins or releases. Enter and Space fire it without pinning. Set pinOnClick="false" to disable pinning, or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for every chart.
