Both scales are continuous, so a point carries its own position on each rather than an index into a list of categories. xLabel and yLabel are not decoration: without them the reader has two numbers and no idea which is which, and on a scatter that is the whole reading. Marks are drawn semi-transparent, because the interesting thing is where they pile up and at full opacity a cluster of forty and a cluster of four are the same solid disc.

Several series

Each series takes its own palette slot and the legend keys them. Two clouds that overlap are the reading, not a defect - it is the case the transparency exists for.

Thinning, and saying so

SVG degrades by the number of NODES, not by the number of readings: the same data as a line is one <path> , and as a scatter it is one node per point. Lighthouse warns at about 800 nodes and errors at about 1400, on a page that may hold four more charts. So past four times the plot's width in marks the series is decimated - and the chart says so on its face, because a quiet decimation leaves the reader counting points that were never drawn. Every reading is still in the data table.
