A running total, bar by bar: what a figure started at, what moved it, and what it ended at. The one chart in the family that cannot be a mode on another.

Default

Each bar carries a value that is added to the running total wherever it sits. A delta , the default, hangs from the level the bar before it left off at; a total stands on zero at the sum reached, which is what makes it a figure in its own right rather than a change. A closing total usually carries value: 0 : it prints the sum, not a change of its own.

Colour never carries it alone

Three meanings, three colours, and none is load-bearing alone: every delta prints its signed change above it and every bar's accessible name says "increase", "decrease" or "total", so the chart survives forced colours, monochrome print and colour-blind readers. The pair uses the palette's lead hues, not --success / --error , because a rise is a direction, not a verdict. Recolour with --ds-chart-positive-color and --ds-chart-negative-color ; totals use --ds-chart-series-default-color .

Subtotals

A subtotal draws exactly like a total : on zero, at the running sum, and the chain carries on past either. The two kinds differ only in what they say to the reader of your data.

Crossing the baseline

A chain that falls past zero opens the axis downwards rather than clipping, and the dashed connector stays at the level the two bars share whichever side of the baseline that is. tickCount (default 5) sets how many value-axis ticks to aim for; they snap to a round step.

Values and connectors

showValues prints each delta's change, with its sign, and each total's running sum above the bar; showConnectors draws the dashed rule from one bar's end to where the next begins. Both are on by default. Turn the values off where the chart sits beside a table that already carries them, and the connectors off where bars are too close for the rule to read.

default
showValues = false
showConnectors = false

Drilling in

interactive turns on the affordance and the keyboard route, and barClicked hands back the datum that was clicked. Every point of the plot answers its nearest step, the step's label on the axis included, so the pointer never crosses ground that opens nothing.

Holding a step

A click on a bar holds it and its tooltip in place, with a pin disc on it and an Unpin control at the top end of the chart. A second click, Esc or a press elsewhere lets go, and barClicked fires either way. Set pinOnClick="false" to keep bars unheld, or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for every chart.

Dimming and the tooltip

dimOthers fades every step but the hovered or focused one, and beside a pin the rest stays down too. It is off by default. tooltip="none" takes the panel away, for a chart whose values are printed beside it; 'item' , the default, opens it on each step. There is no 'axis': each bar is its own category.

Stagger

Steps grow out of their baseline one after another. staggerMs sets the step between two bars, 40 by default, and the whole spread fits inside 260ms, so a long series narrows the step instead of crawling in. 0 brings every step in at once.
