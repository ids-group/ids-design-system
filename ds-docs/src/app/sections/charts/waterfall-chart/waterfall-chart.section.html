A running total, bar by bar: where a figure started, what moved it, where it ended.

Default

  • Each bar's value adds to the running total.
  • A delta (default) hangs from the previous bar's end.
  • A total stands on zero at the sum.
  • A closing total usually carries value: 0: it prints the sum, not a change.

Colour never carries it alone

  • Deltas print a signed change; accessible names say "increase", "decrease" or "total". Both survive forced colours and monochrome.
  • Rise and fall use the palette's lead hues, not --success / --error : a change is a direction, not a verdict.
  • Recolour with --ds-chart-positive-color and --ds-chart-negative-color ; totals use --ds-chart-series-default-color .

Subtotals

A subtotal draws exactly like a total , and the chain continues past either; they differ only in meaning.

Crossing the baseline

Below zero the axis extends instead of clipping. tickCount (default 5) targets a tick count, snapped to a round step.

Values and connectors

showValues prints changes and sums above bars; showConnectors draws dashed rules between them. Both default on; turn off beside a table, or when bars are cramped.

default
showValues = false
showConnectors = false

Drilling in

interactive adds the affordance and keyboard route; barClicked emits the datum. Any point, axis labels included, hits its nearest step.

Holding a step

A click holds a bar and its tooltip; barClicked fires either way. Release and opt-out: Charts foundations.

Dimming and the tooltip

  • tooltip : 'item' (default) opens a panel per step, 'none' removes it.
  • No 'axis': each bar is its own category.
  • dimOthers : Charts foundations.

Stagger

staggerMs delays each bar's growth (40 by default), capped so the spread fits 260ms. 0 brings every step in at once.

Loading

  • loading , no data - total, floating steps, closing total.
  • loading , bars on screen - kept under the ds-loading-overlay scrim and spinner.
