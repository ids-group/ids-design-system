A ring splitting one total into parts, each labelled with its whole-percent share.

Default

Slices run clockwise from twelve o'clock, in order. Shares use largest remainder to total exactly 100, so one may differ from plain rounding by 1.

Legend placement

'bottom' suits a narrow column: the ring stays full size. 'none' only when the parts are named elsewhere.

Centre content

The hole prints centerValue , else the formatted total, captioned by centerLabel . At innerRadius="0" neither renders. A hovered slice takes the centre; [centerFollowsHover]="false" keeps it.

Centre value fit

  • The value never wraps.
  • Wider than the hole, it shrinks to fit, down to --ds-pie-chart-center-value-min-font-size .
  • Past that floor the sum prints in its 'tick' form, unless [centerCompact]="false" .
  • A centerValue string is never shortened.
  • centerPlacement="below" prints the value under the ring at full size.

Legend length

  • maxSlices (off by default) caps the legend by summing the smallest slices into one.
  • That slice is named by otherLabel, in palette slot 8.
  • Kept slices stay in the given order.
nine slices, uncapped
maxSlices 5

Interaction

  • interactive adds hover tint and makes slices controls; the pointer cursor shows on them and on any holdable slice.
  • sliceClicked fires either way.
  • tooltip : 'item' (default) or 'none' .
  • Focus travels the legend items; at legend="none" , the arcs.
  • A hovered or focused legend item opens its arc's tooltip and slides the arc out, as hovering the arc does.
  • Arcs share edges, so the ring enters as one, unstaggered.
  • Keys and dimming: Charts foundations .

Pinning a slice

A click holds the slice and its tooltip. Release and opt-out: Charts foundations .

Palette slots

Colours go by position, so dropping a slice recolours the rest; slot pins one. Slots count from one and wrap past eight.
