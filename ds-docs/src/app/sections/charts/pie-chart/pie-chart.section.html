Default

Slices run clockwise from twelve o'clock in the order you pass them. Shares are settled by largest remainder so they total exactly 100, which is why a slice can print a percentage one off the value you would get by rounding it yourself.

Legend placement

end is the default; bottom is for a narrow column, where the ring would otherwise shrink to make room beside it; none is only safe where the parts are named elsewhere.

Centre content

Wherever there is a hole, the middle prints something: your centerValue when you give one, the formatted total when you do not, with centerLabel captioning it. At innerRadius="0" there is no hole to hold it, so neither renders.

Legend length

maxSlices caps the legend and sums the smallest slices into one, named by otherLabel . Off by default, the tail is summed rather than dropped, and the slices you keep stay in the order you gave them.

Interaction

interactive turns on the pointer cursor and makes each slice a control; sliceClicked fires whether or not it is set. tooltip chooses whether a hovered slice opens a panel — 'item' by default, 'none' to suppress it. dimOthers fades the slices that are not under the pointer, and staggerMs is accepted for parity with the other four charts but has no effect here: the ring arrives as one object, because arcs share their edges and fading them one at a time shows the background through every seam. Hovering or focusing a legend item reads as hovering the arc it names: the same tooltip opens over the ARC, and the rest of the ring dims with it.

Pinning a slice

pinOnClick holds the clicked slice and its tooltip in place, so the reading stays while you look away from the ring. With dimOthers beside it the rest of the ring stays down too. A pushpin marks the held arc and an Unpin button appears above the plot; Esc , a press outside, or clicking the slice again releases it.

Palette slots

Slices take the categorical palette by position, so dropping one recolours everything after it; slot pins a slice to a fixed palette position instead. Slots count from one and wrap past the eighth.

Data table

none , the default, renders no table at all; toggle puts one behind a closed disclosure; visible renders it open. The table is the non-visual route to the numbers, so none belongs only where the same figures are already in a table on the page. Every share column here is on by default, unlike the other charts: partOfWhole starts true on a ring, because a ring IS parts of one whole. Set it false to drop the percentage from the legend, the tooltip and the table alike.
