Tick count

tickCount is a hint; ticks snap to a round step (3 may give 5, 10 may give 8).

Pinning a reading

A click pins the column and tooltip (pin disc, Unpin on the caption line); Escape, a second click or an outside press releases. pointClicked fires either way. dimOthers fades all but the active column (rested hover, focus or pin). pinOnClick="false" disables pinning.

Column values

Turn showValues off for narrow columns or when an open table shows the numbers.

Column colour

Palette colours go in list order, wrapping after eight, so removing a point recolours later ones; a point's own color overrides.

Readings of a different standing

A point with fill="hatched" adds stripes over its colour, marking a projection in greyscale, print and colour-vision deficiency. The caller marks projections.

More than one column per category

Hand data NAMED SERIES for one column per series per category, with a legend; hovering or focusing an item highlights its series. stack : 'none' (default) and grouped side by side, stacked stacked, normalized stacked as shares of the category total. A flat list is one full-band series.

A series has one colour; a flat list colours by position (its marks ARE the categories). A series-wide fill applies to all its columns.

Segments are split by a 2px surface-colour gap, the palette's second encoding for print and colour-vision deficiency. Under normalized only the axis shows shares; segment values stay readings.
