A matrix of readings coloured by magnitude - depot by weekday, hour by date, queue by region.

Default

One reading per cell. Magnitude reads from the STEP between colours, so the heat scale spans the full lightness range (unlike the funnel's mid-range ramp). Cells: --ds-heatmap-chart-cell-size (34px), --ds-heatmap-chart-cell-gap (2px), --ds-heatmap-chart-cell-radius ; labels: --ds-heatmap-chart-label-color , --ds-heatmap-chart-label-font-size .

The chart is as wide as its grid, capped at its container (inline-size: fit-content), and a wider grid scrolls inside it. To fill the container, set inline-size: auto on it from a component stylesheet, or as :root ds-heatmap-chart from a global one.

What has to be there

Colour is the only channel for the quantity, so two things are mandatory: the scale under the grid, and the annex table, always drawn (no dataTable option) because coloured squares alone fail WCAG 1.4.1. Each cell announces row, column: value . dimOthers fades every cell but the one under the pointer; tooltip="none" drops the panel.

Pointing at a scale step raises it and fades every cell outside that step.

A hole is not a low reading

A null draws in --ds-heatmap-chart-missing-color (surface-secondary), not the coldest step, and is left out of the scale. Below, depot F opened on Thursday and nobody reports on Sunday - neither reads as a quiet day.

Fixed scale

Each grid scales to its own readings, so two grids colour the same value differently. min and max pin floor and ceiling; they only widen the scale, never narrow it.

Printing the readings

showValues prints each reading in its cell. Off by default: a dense grid has no room. The glyph takes the cell's colour inverted, legible across a scale running 1.14:1 to 9.07:1 against white.

Drilling in

interactive makes every MEASURED cell a button firing cellClicked with row, column and reading. Unmeasured cells stay inert, so they are no tab trap.

Holding a cell

Clicking a measured cell pins it and its tooltip, with a pin disc and an Unpin control at the chart's top end. A second click, Esc or a press elsewhere releases it; on an interactive heat map cellClicked fires either way. Enter and Space click without pinning. Disable with pinOnClick="false" , or DS_CONFIG.chart.pinOnClick: false for every chart.

Stagger

Cells fade in column by column, left to right. staggerMs sets the step between columns (default 40); the whole spread fits in 260ms, so wide grids get a smaller step. 0 shows the grid at once.
