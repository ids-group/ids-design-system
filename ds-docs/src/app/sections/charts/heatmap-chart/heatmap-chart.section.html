A matrix of readings coloured by magnitude - depot by weekday, hour by date, queue by region.

Default

One reading per cell. Magnitude reads from the step between colours, so the heat scale spans the full lightness range.

  • inline-size: fit-content (default) - as wide as the grid, capped at the container; a wider grid scrolls.
  • inline-size: auto - fills the container. Set it from a component stylesheet, or as :root ds-heatmap-chart globally.

What has to be there

  • Colour is the only channel for the quantity, so the scale and the annex table always draw (WCAG 1.4.1); no dataTable option.
  • Each cell announces row, column: value.
  • Pointing at a scale step raises it and brightens its cells; under dimOthers the rest fade.
  • A hovered cell marks its reading on the scale; [showScaleMarker]="false" drops the marker.
  • tooltip="none" drops the panel; dimming: Charts foundations.

A hole is not a low reading

A null draws in --ds-heatmap-chart-missing-color (surface-secondary), not the coldest step, and is left out of the scale.

Below, depot F opened on Thursday and nobody reports on Sunday.

Fixed scale

Each grid scales to its own readings, so two grids colour the same value differently.

min and max pin floor and ceiling; they only widen the scale.

Printing the readings

  • showValues prints each reading in its cell. Off by default: a dense grid has no room.
  • The glyph takes the cell's colour inverted, legible across a scale running 1.14:1 to 9.07:1 against white.

Drilling in

interactive makes every MEASURED cell a button firing cellClicked with row, column and reading. Unmeasured cells stay inert, so they are no tab trap.

Holding a cell

A click holds a measured cell and its tooltip; on an interactive heat map cellClicked fires either way. Release and opt-out: Charts foundations.

Stagger

  • Cells fade in column by column, left to right.
  • staggerMs sets the step between columns: default 40 , 0 the grid at once.
  • The spread fits in 260ms, so wide grids get a smaller step.
