A matrix of readings coloured by magnitude - depot by weekday, hour by date, queue by region. The least code per unit of insight of anything in the family, and the strictest about what has to sit beside it.

Default

Rows down the side, columns across the top, one reading per cell. Magnitude is read from the STEP between two colours, which is why the heat scale spans the full lightness range rather than a band of near-neighbours. It is a separate scale from the funnel's ramp, which stays in the middle of the range for the opposite reason.

What has to be there

Colour is the only channel carrying the quantity on the grid, so two things are not optional here the way they are on the other charts. The scale under the grid says what a colour is worth - without it the picture is relative warmth with no quantity attached. The annex table is always drawn, never a dataTable choice, because WCAG 1.4.1 is not satisfied by a grid of coloured squares. Every cell also names its own reading to assistive tech as row, column: value .

A hole is not a low reading

A null draws as the surface, not as the heat scale's coldest step, and it is left out of the scale entirely. The coldest step is a real measurement; painting "we never measured this" with it states a figure nobody recorded. Depot F below opened on Thursday and nobody reports on Sunday - neither reads as a quiet day.

Printing the readings

showValues puts each reading inside its own cell. Off by default because a grid dense enough to be worth a heat map has no room for the numbers. The glyph takes the cell's own colour inverted, which is what keeps it legible at both ends of a scale running 1.14:1 to 9.07:1 against white.

Drilling in

interactive makes every MEASURED cell a button and fires cellClicked with the row, the column and the reading. An unmeasured cell stays inert: a grid of buttons with nothing behind them is a tab trap.
