Which region holds what, and in what order.

Four regions, and three of them are components

Everything sits in one of these; dashed ones are app chrome, not per-page. The grid is in Foundations → Layout.

ds-sidenav rail reserved first, outside the grid
ds-topbar global
ds-page-header breadcrumbs · title · meta · actions · tabs
ds-filter-bar only if the page has data to filter
the content, on .page-grid
ds-page-footer optional

The header slots are a reading order, not a set

Slot Place
[breadcrumbs] Above the title
[title-badge] Beside the title
[meta] Under the title
[actions] Right-aligned on the title line
[tabs] Last, on the header's bottom edge

Page actions go nowhere else: no floating button, no row above the header.

Three placement rules that settle most arguments

  • Filters sit directly above the data they filter - between header and content, full width, never in a clipping wrapper, a sidebar or the table's card. A 9+3 split is for a genuine filter RAIL.
  • One primary per region, once - page header, each card, modal actions. Secondary takes outline , dismissive ghost .
  • Supporting information goes under, never beside - hints, counts and timestamps sit under their subject at --space-1 . Beside is for actions. Never a tooltip: it is hover-only.

When content belongs in a card

A card holds a UNIT that could be moved, collapsed or repeated: record summary, KPI tile, form section. Default: no card.

  • Never a full-width table: it has its own frame, so a card double-borders it
