Four regions, and three of them are components

Everything sits in one of these. Dashed: the sidenav (OUTSIDE the page grid) and the topbar (global, not per-page).

ds-sidenav rail reserved first, outside the grid
ds-topbar global
ds-page-header breadcrumbs · title · meta · actions · tabs
ds-filter-bar only if the page has data to filter
the content, on .page-grid
ds-page-footer optional

The header slots are a reading order, not a set

[breadcrumbs] above the title, [title-badge] beside it, [meta] under it, [actions] right-aligned on the title line, [tabs] last, on the header's bottom edge. Page actions go nowhere else: not a floating button, not a row above the header.

Three placement rules that settle most arguments

  • Filters sit directly above the data they filter - between header and content, full width, no wrapper that could clip the overflow popover. Not in a sidebar or the table's card. The 9+3 split is reserved for a genuine filter RAIL.
  • One primary per region, once - page header, each card, modal actions. Two primaries in a region make neither primary. Secondary takes outline , dismissive ghost .
  • Supporting information goes under, never beside - hints, counts and timestamps sit under their subject at --space-1 . Beside is for actions. Never a tooltip: hover-only, it cannot hold what the user needs to act.

When content belongs in a card

A card is for a UNIT that could be moved, collapsed or repeated: a record summary, a KPI tile, a form section. Not for "content": a full-width table has its own frame, and a card double-borders it. Default: no card.
