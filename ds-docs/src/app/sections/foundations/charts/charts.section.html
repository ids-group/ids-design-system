Reading a value without keeping the pointer still

A tooltip that vanishes the moment the pointer moves off the mark cannot be read at high zoom, cannot have its number selected, and cannot be cleared without moving the pointer somewhere else. WCAG 1.4.13 asks for all three, so every chart tooltip here is reachable, selectable and dismissable: leaving a mark clears it after a short grace period rather than at once, arriving on the panel cancels that, and Esc closes it. Once cleared it stays cleared until you visit another mark, so pressing Esc to get the panel off the screen actually gets it off the screen.

pinOnClick goes further on all five charts: clicking a mark holds it, its tooltip, and — with dimOthers — the dimming of everything else, so a comparison survives the pointer leaving the chart entirely. Esc or a press outside releases it, and the click output fires either way, because pinning is a state rather than a handler. On ds-line-chart the pin hangs off the data markers, so Enter and Space pin from the keyboard too.

App-wide chart defaults

Four things are the same decision for every chart in an app rather than a choice per plot, so they resolve through DS_CONFIG : valueFormat (a number reads one way in a product, and a formatter passed per chart drifts as soon as there are two screens), dataTable (whether the numbers behind a plot have a non-visual route is an accessibility policy), and the emptyText / errorText copy. Precedence is the input, then the config, then the built-in default — so binding one on a single chart still wins. 

providers: [
  provideDsConfig({
    chart: {
      valueFormat: (value) => value.toLocaleString('de-DE'),
      dataTable: 'visible',
      emptyText: 'Нет данных',
      errorText: 'Не удалось загрузить',
    },
  }),
]
