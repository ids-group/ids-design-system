Clipping text

styles/text-clip clips text without cutting a glyph, whatever the font. single-line-ellipsis ends one line in an ellipsis and clips the inline axis only; inline-axis clips a row sideways and leaves the block axis visible; line-clamp($lines) clamps a block to $lines lines at line-height: normal .
