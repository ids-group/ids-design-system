Clipping text

styles/text-clip clips without cutting a glyph, in any font.

  • single-line-ellipsis - one line, ellipsis; clips the inline axis only.
  • inline-axis - clips a row sideways; block axis stays visible.
  • line-clamp($lines) - $lines lines at line-height: normal .
