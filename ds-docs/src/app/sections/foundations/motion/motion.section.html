Chart entrances

A chart times each arrival by what arrives. --ds-motion-chart-mark-enter-duration (400ms, on --ease-emphasized-decelerate) is a mark that fades or settles in place; -grow-duration (600ms, on --ease-settle ) a column or bar growing out of its baseline; -sweep-duration (600ms) a ring turning in or a line drawing on; -detail-duration (180ms) what follows a mark, its value label or marker. A series' arrivals spread across 260ms, however many marks it has. Under reduced motion every mark appears at once, delays included.
