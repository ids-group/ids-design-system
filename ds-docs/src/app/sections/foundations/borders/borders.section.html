Control boundaries

Every control is told apart from what it sits on at 3:1 or more, by one of two rules.

  • A choice inside a track (ds-segmented): the track and its segments draw no border; the selected segment is a solid --segmented-selected fill, at least 3:1 against the track, with its label in --segmented-selected-on . joined has no track and keeps its segment borders.
  • A standalone control (ds-input, ds-select , ds-textarea , ds-number-stepper , ds-toggle-button , ds-checkbox , ds-radio , ds-switch ): a --border-field boundary at rest, at least 3:1 against the page, a card, an overlay and their secondary fills; the selected or checked state takes the accent.
