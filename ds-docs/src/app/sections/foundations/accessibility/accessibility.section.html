Rules every component keeps, so a screen built from them keeps them with no opt-in.

Pointer target: 24px

Every icon-only control takes the pointer over at least --hit-xs (24px, WCAG 2.5.8), however small its icon is drawn. The box keeps its size, so nothing around it moves and its fills stay as drawn; a transparent layer takes the pointer around it, and its focus ring is drawn on the edge of that 24px target.

Design Conversion rate
Token
--hit-xs: 24px. Raise it, to 44px for a touch screen, and every icon-only control's target follows.
Your own control
@use 'styles/target' and @include target.min-size last in its rule, after its own focus style. A control at least 24px by its own size grows by nothing.
Beside text
$ring-on-target: false keeps the ring on the icon, for a control set a few pixels from a label, such as ds-info-hint; the target still grows.
