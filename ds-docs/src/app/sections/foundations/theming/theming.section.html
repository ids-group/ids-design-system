Colour has three layers: a theme-independent base palette , theme role maps (light / dark), and optional brand overlays. Light is the default; dark is opt-in. Components read only semantic roles, so a theme or brand re-skins everything.

Activation

One attribute on <html> switches the whole document, not a subtree.

  • nothing - light (default)
  • <html data-theme="dark"> - dark
  • <html data-theme="light"> - explicit light

<html data-neutrals="brand"> re-tints the neutral ramp from --primary-h; light theme only, dark keeps its own ramp.

There is no automatic prefers-color-scheme switch . To follow the OS, add provideDsTheme({{ '{' }} followSystem: true {{ '}' }}) : it sets data-theme at startup and on each OS change, not on the server. A data-theme already on <html> at startup wins over the OS.

Three layers

Base palette (themes/_base-palette) — theme-independent primitives at :root : hue ramps + HSL channels, --static-ink (never flips), fonts.
Theme / roles (themes/_semantic + themes/<t>/) — the semantic role map (--primary, --surface-* , --text-* , --neutral-* , --focus-ring ). Light at :root , dark at :root[data-theme='dark'] .
Component / scale (styles/_tokens, *.component.scss) — --space-* , --radius-* , --ds-* , motion. Theme-independent.

Hard rule: components never read base-palette primitives (--blue-600), only Tier-2 roles (--primary, --text-secondary ) or component tokens.

Sibling themes, not a patch

Light and dark are sibling role maps over one base palette and role structure. The dark block declares the complete token set, so :root[data-theme='dark'] replaces light, not patches it. On-fill text is white , or --static-ink on light fills; neither flips with the neutrals.

Writing your own theme or brand

A brand is a thin overlay on <html> ([data-brand="X"]): re-point palette channels (--blue-h/s/l, read by --primary-h/s/l ) and maybe a few roles. A full theme is a new sibling role map.

You author on light, the default. Palette and channel overrides carry into dark. Role overrides (--primary, --primary-h/s/l , --surface-* ) lose to the dark block; restate them under [data-theme='dark'][data-brand="X"] .

Leave --primary-hover / --primary-pressed to the DS color-mix() derivation (toward black in light, toward white in dark). Full guide: themes/README.md .

App-level defaults — provideDsConfig

Tokens set looks; provideDsConfig sets inputs an app uses everywhere (a house size="sm", [stickyScrollbar]="true" on every table). Precedence: input → config → built-in. Provide at the root, on a Route or a component; calls merge per section, the later winning per key. provideDsIntl merges the same way. 

{{ configSnippet }}

control.size is not the same axis as [data-density] . Density rewrites hit targets and padding in CSS per route; size picks one of three declared control sizes. Both can apply.

Locale and wording

Numbers and dates follow DsConfig.locale, then Angular's LOCALE_ID (charts, percentages, date picker, file sizes). Library strings follow DS_INTL , English until you provide provideDsIntl . Set both once at the root; a factory returning a signal switches language live. The demo uses locale: 'de-DE' . A custom range also receives (0, 0, 0) for an empty list, from the pager and from ds-table's announcement.

Zeroing the three roles squares most surfaces. Menu panel, toast, split-button and button-group segments, sidenav footer and colour-picker swatches read the scale directly; zero --radius-* too to reach them. Leave --radius-pill alone: switch tracks, avatars and status dots are round by shape, and the switch track resolves the pill token, so it stays round with the scale zeroed.
