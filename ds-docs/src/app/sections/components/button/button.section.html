Two axes: color (hue family) × variant (solid · tonal · outline · ghost · text); loudness is the variant (solid > tonal > outline/ghost).

type defaults to 'button' (never submits); use type="submit" to submit. Overlay triggers bind ariaHasPopup, ariaExpanded , ariaControls ; these and ariaLabel land on the inner control. Labelled groups: ds-button-group.

Only solid and tonal paint a fill. outline , ghost , text show the surface beneath; on a photo or gradient set --ds-button-bg-color .

Color × variant

Defaults: color="primary" variant="solid" . ⚠ cells work (with a dev warning) but read poorly: grey solid looks disabled; outline/ghost success/warning lack contrast; use solid/tonal there.

The text column is ⚠: it duplicates .link on <a> /<button>. Use variant="text" only for loading , fullWidth , (pressed) or icons on that look.

secondary (orange) and tertiary (violet) are independent of --primary ; recolour via --secondary-h/s/l , --tertiary-h/s/l .
