Interactive tree

  • TreeNode[] : id , label , icon? , children? , disabled? .
  • [(expandedIds)] , [(selectedId)] - two-way; (nodeSelected) emits the whole node.
  • A click on a parent selects and toggles it.
  • An icon never moves the label: a row without children shows it in the caret's place, a row with children left of the caret.

Trailing content, hover and multiple selection

  • <ng-template dsTreeTrailing let-node> draws at the end of every row: a badge, a count, a small icon button. A click inside it neither selects nor toggles the row; its controls are tab stops on the focused row only.
  • (nodeHover) emits the node under the pointer or newly focused, and null when the pointer leaves the tree.
  • selection="multiple" binds [(selectedIds)] : Shift-click, Ctrl-click or Shift+Space adds or removes a row; a plain click or Enter selects that row alone.

Two-line label

  • <ng-template dsTreeLabel let-node> replaces the row's label. The context carries node , level , selected and disabled .
  • The row grows to the label's height. Each line here cuts with an ellipsis and shows its full text in a tooltip only while it is clipped, with dsTooltipWhenClipped .
  • The label's text is the row's accessible name.
