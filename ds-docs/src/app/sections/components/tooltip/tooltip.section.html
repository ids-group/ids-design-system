Pin on click

dsTooltipPinOnClick : opens with no delay; a click keeps it open after the pointer leaves. Opt in only where the click does nothing else.

  • Closes on a click outside, Esc, blur or a second click on the trigger.
  • Without it - a click closes the tip.
  • Any scroll (window or container) closes the tip, pinned or not, and a ds-menu-item submenu.

Info hint

<ds-info-hint> : an icon button named by ariaLabel; its text shows on hover and focus, pins on click.

  • icon="help" (default) - a question mark.
  • icon="info" - an "i".
  • Any other DS icon name, such as icon="warn" ; colour it with --ds-info-hint-color .
  • Rich content: put it inside the tag, or pass a template to [content] . Keep it non-interactive; a screen reader reads it as flat text.

Definition term

dsDefinitionTerm="…" : a dotted underline; the definition shows on hover and focus, a click pins it.

  • On a <span> - adds tabindex="0" , role="button" , Enter / Space ; a <button> has them.
  • [pinOnClick]="false" - where the click already acts, as on a sortable ds-table header.

Trigger states

  • Hover - the underline turns solid and darker.
  • Press - tints the term.
  • Pinned - the accent until the tip closes; the trigger carries data-ds-tooltip-pinned , styleable on any dsTooltipPinOnClick host.
  • No state changes the term's size; the line around it stays put.

Same cue on your own trigger, from styles/pinned :

  • pinned.cue() - pinned.
  • pinned.cue-hover() - hover.
  • pinned.cue-pressed() - press.
  • $underline: false - on each, for an icon trigger.

Only while clipped

dsTooltipWhenClipped shows the tip only while the host is clipped, re-tested on every open.

  • No dsTooltip - shows the host's own text.
  • A selector - aims the clip test and the text at the match; no match, no tip.
  • Bound - a selector or false.
