Pin on click

dsTooltipPinOnClick opens at once on hover and focus, and a click keeps the tip open after the pointer leaves. A click outside, Escape or blur closes it; a second click on the trigger too. Opt in only where the click does nothing else: without it a click closes the tip.

Trigger states

Hover makes the underline solid and darker; a press tints the term; a pinned tip paints its trigger in the accent until it closes. The pinned trigger carries data-ds-tooltip-pinned , which any dsTooltipPinOnClick host can style. No state changes the size of the term, so the line around it never moves.

Only while clipped

dsTooltipWhenClipped shows the tip only while the host is clipped, re-tested on every open. Without dsTooltip it shows the host's own text. dsTooltipTextSelector aims the clip test and text at a descendant; no match, no tip. A clipped host that also has a dsTooltip leads the tip with its own text in bold.

A clipped host that cannot take focus (label in a button, cell of a clickable row) opens when keyboard focus lands on its nearest focusable ancestor; blur, a click or any key closes it. That ancestor must be a Tab-reachable control: native, or a widget role (button, link, tab, option, row, combobox). A focusable container (tab panel, scroll region), negative tabindex or click opens nothing. Only the first clipped value per control opens.
