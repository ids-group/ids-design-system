Alignment

align="start" (default) aligns to the trigger's left edge; align="end" to its right, for triggers near the viewport's right edge. Only for top and bottom placement.

Align start

Panel left-aligned to the trigger.

Align end

Placement

placement sets the preferred side: 'top' , 'bottom' (default), 'left' , or 'right' . Flips when space runs out.

Bottom
