Alignment

align="start" (default) anchors the panel to the trigger's left edge; align="end" anchors to the right. Use "end" when the trigger sits near the right edge of the viewport.

Align start

Panel left-aligned to the trigger.

Align end

Panel right-aligned to the trigger.

Placement

placement controls which side of the trigger the panel prefers. Accepts 'top', 'bottom' (default), 'left' , or 'right' . The overlay flips automatically if there is not enough space.

Top

Appears above the trigger.

Bottom

Appears below the trigger.

Left

Appears to the left.

Right

Appears to the right.

Arrow

Add arrow to render a caret pointing at the trigger. Combine with placement for directional emphasis.
