A vertical stack of rows with [leading] / [trailing] slots, [clickable] items and a [selected] state. Dividers are on by default; [dividers]="false" removes them.

List with leading and trailing icons

Use the [leading] and [trailing] slots for icons or avatars. Tokens: --ds-list-divider-color and --ds-list-item-* (padding-y/x, gap, hover-bg-color, selected-bg/label/icon-color, label/subtitle-color).

Selected state

A [clickable] row is a button that emits activated; moving the selection is the consumer's job. [selected] tints the row and sets aria-current. [disabled] disables the row's button; projected controls keep their own state.

A row with its own control

[leading] and [trailing] sit outside the row's button, so a row can carry its own controls (a bulk-action checkbox) and still be the target that selects - two independent targets.

A checkbox inside a <button> would be invalid HTML, bubble into activated and lose its checked state for screen readers. The host is role="list", not listbox: a listbox option cannot hold interactive content.
