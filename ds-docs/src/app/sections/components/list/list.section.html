Vertical rows, optionally clickable and selected.

List with leading and trailing icons

[leading] and [trailing] take icons or avatars. Dividers are on until [dividers]="false" . Tokens: --ds-list-divider-color and --ds-list-item-* (padding-y/x, gap, hover-bg-color, selected-bg/label/icon-color, label/subtitle-color).

Selected state

A [clickable] row is a button emitting activated ; you move the selection. [selected] tints the row and sets aria-current. [disabled] disables the row's button, not projected controls.

A row with its own control

[leading] and [trailing] sit outside the row's button, so a row can hold its own control (a bulk-action checkbox) and still select.

  • A checkbox inside a <button> is invalid HTML, bubbles into activated and loses its checked state for screen readers.
  • The host is role="list", not listbox , whose options cannot hold interactive content.
