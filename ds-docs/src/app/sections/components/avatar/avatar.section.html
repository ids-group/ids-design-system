Initials or image avatars, plus stacked groups.

Sizes

imageUrl shows an image ahead of initials and fallbackIcon .

Group

Shows the first max avatars (default 4) and a +N chip for the rest, counted from the projected avatars or from total. tint="neutral" gives an avatar the chip look.

Status dots

  • status - adds a presence dot.
  • Person and status go in ariaLabel ("Alice Brown, Online"): a labelled avatar is role="img" , so the dot's label is not read.
  • No ariaLabel - decorative.

Fallback icon

Shown when neither imageUrl nor initials is set.

Seeded tint

A seed gives a deterministic hsl(H 50% 62%) tint with white initials, identical across apps. avatarTint and initialsFrom (also takes one full name) are exported.

Input Effect
name Derives the initials and seeds the tint. The tint hashes the name, never the initials: Alice Brown and Adam Bell (both "AB") get 198° and 356°.
seed Tint seed for an id or email; defaults to name.
initials Explicit text; wins over derived initials.
initialsRule Replaces the name-to-initials rule. Fallback: provideDsConfig({ avatar: { initials } }) , then the first letters of the first two words (Latin script; Spanish wants MG for "María José García López").
avoidHues Hue arcs the seeded tint skips, such as a brand's pink and red.
tint A { bg, fg } pair or 'neutral' ; replaces the seeded tint.
