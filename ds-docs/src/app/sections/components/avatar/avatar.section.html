Status dots

Pass status to show a presence indicator in the bottom-right corner of the avatar.

Fallback icon

When neither imageUrl nor initials are provided, the avatar renders the fallbackIcon.

Seeded tint

Pass seed (an email or name) for a deterministic OKLCH colour — the same person always gets the same tint across apps, and the initials auto-pick black or white for contrast. The helpers avatarTint and initialsFrom (which also accepts a single full name) are exported for use outside the component.
