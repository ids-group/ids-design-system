Read-only

[readOnly]="true" shows an uneditable value on --surface-secondary .

Character counter

[maxLength] caps length natively and shows a current/max counter at the right of the hint row.

maxLengthMode="soft" allows text past the limit; the form decides whether to block saving. Past it:

  • The counter turns red and the excess is highlighted.
  • The field sets aria-invalid, described by the counter.

[countTrimmed]="true" excludes leading and trailing whitespace from count, over-limit state and highlight, for values stored trimmed. Inner whitespace counts.

Autosize

[autosize]="true" grows with content between [minRows] and [maxRows] . resize sets the native handle (default vertical ); ignored under autosize .

Selection API

selectionStart / selectionEnd , setSelectionRange(start, end, direction?) and select() forward to the native textarea; toolbars need not touch its DOM.
