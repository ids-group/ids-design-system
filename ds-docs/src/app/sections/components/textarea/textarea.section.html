Read-only

[readOnly]="true" renders the field on a muted --surface-secondary background — value is visible but not editable.

Character counter

[maxLength] caps input length natively and shows a live current/max counter below the field — right-aligned, aligned with the hint.

Autosize

[autosize]="true" grows the textarea with its content (forces resize="none"). Bound by [minRows] / [maxRows] .
