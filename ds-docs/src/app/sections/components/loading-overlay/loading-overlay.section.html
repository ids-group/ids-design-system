Covers a region with a spinner and optional label during async work.

Interactive demo

Sits over its host; toggling never unmounts the content. [blur]="false" drops the backdrop blur; spinnerSize takes sm · md (default) · lg . Tokens: --ds-loading-overlay-scrim-color , -label-color , -radius (default 0; match the covered container) and -enter-keyframes/-duration/-easing/-scale .

The scrim stops the keyboard too

The scrim blocks the pointer; while loading, the covered content goes inert (not tabbable, out of the accessibility tree). aria-busy stays on the host.

Part of the content: offset the top with insetTop , set [inertContent]="false" , mark the covered subtree inert yourself.
