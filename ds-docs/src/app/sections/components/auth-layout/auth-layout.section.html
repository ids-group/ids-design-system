On a short screen the chrome compresses, not the card

  • Block padding, column gap and masthead gap clamp against svh, full size at 800px of viewport height; type and tile stay fixed.
  • The clamps read the real viewport: shorten the window to see them.

svh , not dvh , so the column does not re-space as a mobile URL bar hides. A fixed --ds-auth-layout-padding-block or -gap opts that dimension out.

A too-tall card scrolls; the chrome stays in flow (no position: absolute ), never overlapping it.

Why it centres with auto margins

Auto margins, not align-items: center: in a fixed-height parent (--ds-auth-layout-min-height: 100%) a too-tall form pins to the top padding and scrolls.

The notch needs one line from YOUR app

  • env(safe-area-inset-*) padding clears a landscape notch; ds-bottom-sheet, the home indicator.
  • The insets are 0 until the host document opts in via the viewport meta tag in your index.html. 
<!-- your index.html -->
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover" />

Without it every inset reads 0. This site sets it.
