On a short screen the chrome compresses, not the card

Block padding, column gap and masthead gap clamp against svh , full size at 800px of viewport height; type and tile stay fixed. Shorten the window to see it.

  • svh , not dvh : the column does not re-space as a mobile URL bar hides
  • A fixed --ds-auth-layout-padding-block or -gap opts that dimension out
  • A too-tall card scrolls; the chrome stays in flow, never overlapping it

Why it centres with auto margins

Auto margins, not align-items: center: in a fixed-height parent (--ds-auth-layout-min-height: 100%) a too-tall form pins to the top padding and scrolls.
