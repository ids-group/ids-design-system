Screen readers, and what the service owns

  • ToastService announces via the CDK LiveAnnouncer: NVDA and JAWS ignore a live region born with text.
  • Errors and warnings - assertive; others - polite. A burst is one announcement.
  • A hand-placed <ds-toast> has no role or aria-live: use the service.

The service lifts the stack above later modals, drawers and confirms; --layer-toast cannot (no z-index in the top layer).
