Page navigation for tabular data, with an ellipsis once there are more than 7 pages.

The <nav> is named by DS_INTL.pagination.label and the current page button carries aria-current="page". Previous, next, page and rows-per-page labels come from DS_INTL.pagination.*; override them to localise. Tokens: --ds-pagination-* (button bg/label/border and hover, active bg/label and hover, radius, label font).

Default

12 pages, current page 4: two ellipses bracket the centred range, one after 1 and one before 12.

States showcase

loading disables every control, masks the range label with a skeleton and sets aria-busy on the nav. With total 0 the label reads "Showing 0 of 0" (DS_INTL.pagination.range(0, 0, 0), so your own range handles that call), every button is disabled and none is current.

Ranges

Ellipsis kicks in once the total exceeds 7 items. With more pages, the centred window stays 3 items wide; ellipses fill the gap on either side as needed.

Rows per page

The rows-per-page select is on by default; [showPageSize]="false" hides it when the host fixes the size. Choices come from pageSizeOptions (default [10, 25, 50, 100]); a pageSize outside them is merged in. Changing the size resets currentPage to 1.

Layout and size

layout : 'grouped' (default) keeps label and buttons together at natural width; 'spread' goes full width with the label at one end, for a pager under a table. Unset, size falls back to DS_CONFIG.pagination.size , then DS_CONFIG.control.size , then 'sm' . Unset, layout falls back to DS_CONFIG.pagination.layout , then 'grouped' . Both also reach the pager inside ds-table .
