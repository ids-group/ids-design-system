The scaffold for the screens in front of the login — counterpart to ds-app-layout , which only ever covers what sits behind it.

Sign-in, forgot password, reset and MFA are the same four parts every time: a brand lockup, an optional tagline, one card holding the form, and a support line underneath. The brand row is the sidenav's row — same brand / logoLetter / [dsBrandName] contract, and the same letter-mark tile — so the mark a user meets on the login screen is the mark that greets them in the rail afterwards.

Everything inside the card is yours: the default slot projects straight into it, and the shell deliberately leaves your headings and form alone so its type scale never fights your screen's. The support line goes into [dsAuthSupport] and renders as the page's contentinfo landmark; leave it out and the footer collapses.

Why it centres with auto margins

The full-height box is the shell's own host, and there is only one of it — the hand-rolled version this replaces nested a 100vh wrapper inside a 100dvh one, which scrolls a page whose content fits. Inside it the column is centred with auto margins rather than centre alignment. On a normal page the two are the same, because the host is free to grow and a tall form simply lengthens it. They differ once the host cannot grow — when you embed the shell by setting its min-height token to 100% in a fixed-height parent. There, centre alignment puts a form taller than the box above the scroll origin, out of reach of any scrolling; auto margins collapse to zero instead, so the form pins to the top padding edge and scrolls from there. Both demos above are the real component with that token lowered to fit this page.
