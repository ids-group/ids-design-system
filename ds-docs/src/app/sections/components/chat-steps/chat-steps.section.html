An agent's steps, in the [steps] slot of a chat message: small markers, small text and tight rows, folded into the reply by the message.

Step status

status draws the marker: done, running , pending (a dashed ring and muted text, for a step planned but not reached), waiting or error . A screen reader hears it before the heading.

Waiting for the reader

waiting is a step that needs the reader's answer before it goes on: a grey filled marker with a pause glyph, spoken as "Waiting for your approval". Put the answer buttons under its heading; the marker stays beside the heading.

Steps arriving

Every step is planned up front and turns running, then done. Inside a ds-chat-message , steps that arrive grow in, and enter="stagger" brings a plan given at once in one step after another.
