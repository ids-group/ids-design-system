Mobile-first panel anchored to the bottom edge — the touch equivalent of a drawer / modal. Drag the grab strip down or tap the scrim to dismiss; Esc also closes. Body scroll locks while open. On wider viewports it caps its width and centres.

Anatomy

  • Grab strip — draggable handle at the top; drag down past threshold to dismiss.
  • Title — optional, via [title].
  • Body — default <ng-content>; scrolls if taller than the sheet.
  • Actions — [actions] slot, pinned footer.
  • Dismiss — scrim tap, Esc, drag-down, or setting open=false; each emits (closed).
