Snap points

Pass [snapPoints]="[0.35, 0.65, 0.9]" to enable multi-stop snapping. The sheet opens at the first stop; dragging snaps to the nearest stop; dragging below the lowest stop closes. Heights are fractions of viewport height (0–1), provided in ascending order. Omitting the input preserves the original single-height behaviour.

The home indicator needs one line from YOUR app

The sheet pads its bottom by env(safe-area-inset-bottom) so its last action does not sit under the home indicator. That inset reads 0 until the host document opts in , and a library cannot do it for you — it lives in the viewport meta tag of your index.html . Same requirement as ds-auth-layout , where it is spelled out. 

<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover" />
