Mobile-first panel on the bottom edge, the touch equivalent of a drawer / modal.

Live demo

  • Closes on a drag of the grab strip down, a scrim tap or Esc.
  • Body scroll locks while open.
  • Wide viewports centre it, capped at --ds-bottom-sheet-max-width .
  • position: fixed : a transformed ancestor contains it.

[(open)] drives visibility; the [actions] slot holds footer buttons. (closed) fires after the leave animation, with open already false : for post-close work only.

Snap points

[snapPoints]="[0.35, 0.65, 0.9]" : ascending fractions of viewport height (0-1). Unset - one height.

  • Opens at the first stop.
  • A drag snaps to the nearest; below the lowest, it closes.
Open with snap points

Drag the handle up or down to snap between 35 %, 65 %, and 90 % of the viewport height. Drag past the lowest stop or flick down to close.

35 % — compact filter panel 65 % — medium with sort options 90 % — full sheet with map preview

The home indicator needs one line from YOUR app

The bottom padding env(safe-area-inset-bottom) reads 0 until your index.html viewport meta adds viewport-fit=cover (see ds-auth-layout ). 

<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover" />
