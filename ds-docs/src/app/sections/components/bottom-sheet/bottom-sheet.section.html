Mobile-first panel anchored to the bottom edge — the touch equivalent of a drawer / modal. Drag the grab strip down or tap the scrim to dismiss; Esc also closes. Body scroll locks while open. On wider viewports it caps its width (--ds-bottom-sheet-max-width) and centres. The sheet is position: fixed, so a transformed ancestor contains it.

Live demo

Two-way [(open)] drives visibility; the [actions] slot holds footer buttons. (closed) fires after the leave animation, when open is already false; use it for post-close work only.

Snap points

Pass [snapPoints]="[0.35, 0.65, 0.9]" to enable multi-stop snapping. The sheet opens at the first stop; dragging snaps to the nearest stop; dragging below the lowest stop closes. Heights are fractions of viewport height (0–1), provided in ascending order. Without snapPoints the sheet has a single height.

Open with snap points

Drag the handle up or down to snap between 35 %, 65 %, and 90 % of the viewport height. Drag past the lowest stop or flick down to close.

The home indicator needs one line from YOUR app

The sheet pads its bottom by env(safe-area-inset-bottom), which reads 0 until your index.html viewport meta opts in with viewport-fit=cover (see ds-auth-layout). 

<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover" />
