One horizontal track with one fill: a task's completion, or a measurement on a known scale.

What the bar is

The two readings are the same drawing and a different claim, and semantics is which one you are making. 'progress' , the default, is how far a task has got and carries role="progressbar" . 'meter' is a measurement on a known scale — a rate, a score, a count against a ceiling — which has no beginning and no completion, and carries role="meter" . 'none' draws the bar and hides it from assistive technology, for a cell that prints its figure beside it.

This is not cosmetic. A table of two hundred rates left at the default announces two hundred progress bars, and the row already said the number.

In a table cell

A chart is a block with a caption, a legend and an annex table; in a 60px cell that chrome is the whole cell. This is the mark on its own. max is the ceiling the column is read against — usually the column's own largest value, so the longest bar fills its track and the rest are its share. The figure is printed beside it, so the bars are semantics="none" .

Give the cell a width

The bar fills whatever box it is in, so the column decides how wide it is. A box sized to its content — width: 'content' on ds-table , or a shrink-to-fit flex or grid item — is sized by what its contents contribute intrinsically, and a bar that is 100% of its cell contributes nothing: one alone in such a cell comes out 0px wide. So the host carries a min-inline-size of 3rem , which --ds-bar-min-width overrides. Give it a width of your own where the bar should be a fixed size — the table above puts it in a 64px grid track.

Colour

variant is the semantic route and should be the first reach. The fill otherwise takes --ds-bar-fill-color , so a whole column is coloured once in a stylesheet rather than once per row; the color input overrides both where the colour carries meaning on one row. --ds-bar-height , --ds-bar-track-color , --ds-bar-target-color and --ds-bar-target-width set the rest, and --ds-bar-radius rounds the track and the fill together - a pill by default, square corners at var(--radius-0) .

Edge cases

A value past max fills the track rather than overflowing it, a negative one draws nothing, and a max of zero or less falls back to 100 — a scale with no width has no fraction to draw. indeterminate is ignored unless semantics is 'progress' : only a task has an unknown duration.
